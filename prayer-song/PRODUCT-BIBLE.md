## Persona

None. The brand speaks as "we", plainly and warmly. No named songwriter, no invented team member,
no founder story. (The Find Your Devotion chat uses Pastor Phillip; this offer does not, unless TS
decides otherwise.)

Voice rules: speak like a kind parish friend, not a marketer. Short sentences. Concrete domestic
images (the kitchen table, the school run, the hospital corridor, the empty chair). Reverent, never
syrupy. Never hype.

## Mechanism

**A prayer that is sung keeps being prayed.** A spoken prayer is said once and is gone. A song with
her name in it gets played in the car, at the bedside, on the morning of the surgery, at the
graveside, and every time it plays, she is prayed for again, by name. The buyer is not buying music.
She is having a prayer set down so it can be prayed over and over, by her and by everyone she sends it to.

Why it needs no proof: every Catholic already knows a hymn she has sung all her life and a prayer
she has half forgotten. The Church has always sung what it most wanted to keep.

## Audience

Catholic women 45+. Worried about family: adult children drifting from the Church, marriages under
strain, grandchildren, a sick parent or husband, someone who has died. She prays for people by name
already. See ../CLAUDE.md (Avatar) and ../LEARNINGS.md for her language.

## What she chooses (the survey form, 4 steps, modelled on prayersong.com/create)

Changed 2026-10-05: a step by step form with a progress bar, pills and Back / Next. Not a chat.

1. **The person:** who it is for (husband, wife, son, daughter, grandchild, mother, father, friend, herself, someone who has passed) and their first name, with how it is said if needed
2. **The prayer:** one intention (healing, protection, strength, peace, a return to the faith, a marriage, comfort in grief, thanksgiving), plus one optional line the song should say
3. **The sound:** style, eight options locked 2026-10-05 after TS listened to all of them (traditional hymn, gentle guitar, gospel choir, country gospel, lullaby, piano ballad, folk hymn, sacred choir) and voice (a woman's, a man's, choose for me)
4. **Where to send it:** her email and her own first name, then checkout

The optional line is free text: it goes through the safety check, and a diagnosis is never turned into a lyric.

## Components (what she gets)

1. **The Prayer Song**: written for one person, their name sung in every chorus
2. **The Private Song Page**: hers alone, to play, download and share with family
3. **The Full Lyrics**: every word, to read and pray along with
4. **Scripture at the heart of it**: each song is built on a verse chosen for what she is praying for (Douay-Rheims)

## Song structure (locked 2026-10-05, from the 4 minute render test TS approved)

About 3:54 in ten parts. The lyric writer must fill exactly this shape, about 34 sung lines:

| Part | Length | Lines |
|---|---|---|
| Intro (instrumental) | 10s | 0 |
| Verse 1 | 26s | 4 |
| Chorus (the name, twice) | 28s | 6 |
| Verse 2 | 26s | 4 |
| Chorus | 28s | 6 |
| Interlude (instrumental) | 12s | 0 |
| Bridge (built on the verse of Scripture) | 24s | 4 |
| Verse 3 | 26s | 4 |
| Final chorus (chorus plus two closing lines) | 40s | 8 |
| Outro (amen) | 14s | 1 |

Render cost at this length: about $0.59 a song, made in about 26 seconds.

## One name or two (decided by TS, 2026-10-07)

A song carries ONE first name, or TWO (a husband and wife, two children, a daughter and her husband, the buyer and her spouse). Never more than two; families and groups are not offered. The form's first step has an optional "second name" box with a dropdown for who that person is; it is not offered when the song is for the buyer herself or for someone who has passed. With two names, each is sung at least once in every chorus and both appear in the title or the outro, and the automatic check requires BOTH to be heard. Copy must not say "for one person"; say "by name". Wherever `{name}` appears in copy it may be "Daniel and Mary", so it must never be the subject of a singular verb.

## Bonuses (built 2026-10-06, delivered as PDFs on the song page, no invented dollar values)

1. **The Keepsake Lyric Sheet**: her song's lyrics with the loved one's name, set as a printable page to frame or tuck in a Bible
2. **The Nine Day Prayer Companion**: nine days of prayer to pray alongside the song, one page a day (the document itself never calls itself a novena)
3. **The Daily Prayer Card**: a printable card for the fridge or the bedside

## Personalisation tokens

**Collected:** relationship, name, name pronunciation, intention, optional note, style, voice, buyer email, buyer first name.
**Derived:** pronouns, the verse, the song title, the musical setting, memorial wording.
**Never asked:** surname, date of birth, address, diagnosis or medical detail, money amounts.

## Standing rules for all copy

- No em dashes, no en dashes. Hyphens only inside compound words.
- No testimonials, star ratings, review counts, customer numbers, "as seen on", or press logos. None
  exist. Proof slots stay empty, or the page says plainly that this is new.
- No invented urgency: no countdowns, no "sale ends", no limited spots.
- No health, healing or outcome claims. The song is a prayer FOR healing, never a thing that heals.
  No promise that a prayer will be answered, a child will return, a marriage will mend.
- Sanctoria angle policy: use peace, blessed, blessing, grace, protection, faith, sacred, devotion.
  Never: bad luck, curse, evil, hex, Jezebel, spiritual attack, spiritual warfare.
- Meta personal attributes: never assert the reader's own condition ("your sick husband", "you are
  grieving"). Use third person and community framing ("many mothers pray for a son who has drifted").
- How it is made: TS removed the "How is each song made?" FAQ entry on 2026-10-05, so the landing page no longer
  mentions AI assisted production. What still holds: copy must never say or imply a human singer, songwriter,
  band or studio team made the song, and never deny that AI tools are used if a buyer asks.
- Sample songs on the page are labelled as samples. They are never presented as a customer's song.
- Never say or imply the song is sung in a loved one's voice or in the style of a named artist.
- Never mention Divine Grace Gift.
- Delivery wording: "within 24 hours". Never imply a human will hand write it over days.
