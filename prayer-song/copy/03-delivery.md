FLAGS
1. Empty {buyer}. Both emails open with "{buyer}, {name}'s prayer song...". With {buyer} and its comma removed the sentence begins with {name}, which is already capitalised, so it still reads correctly. The builder should also strip the space after the comma. {buyer} is not used on any page.
2. The myself branch. When she is both buyer and subject, lines such as "{name} is prayed for again" and "the people who pray for {name} too" read in the third person about herself. They are not wrong, but the email would open "Mary, Mary's prayer song is ready." The builder should drop {buyer} when relationship is myself. If variants are wanted for that branch, say so and I will write them.
3. The passed branch. "Sung over {name} by name" and "prayed for again" are appropriate for someone who has died (Catholics pray for the dead), and nothing on these pages says the person is ill, alive or present. With the optional memorial phrases from 02-survey.md the SUB reads "A prayer for eternal rest and peace, sung over Margaret by name", which works.
4. Ready page H is {title} alone, with the eyebrow saying it is ready. The brief asked for {name} in the H; the song title is derived with the name in it per the bible, and {name} appears twice in the SUB directly beneath. If a title can ever lack the name, change H to "{name}'s prayer song is ready" and move {title} to the eyebrow.
5. "Someone listens and means it" (ready SUB) keeps the page honest that a recording does not pray by itself, matching the landing page. The emails say "{name} is prayed for again" after "whenever the prayer is on your heart", which carries the same sense. No line promises an outcome.
6. The review page and the delay email say plainly that the song did not pass the check and is being made again. That is what the delivery pipeline does (retry on a failed name or lyric check). If a song can also be held for other reasons, such as the safety check on the optional note, this wording is still true enough ("being made again") but TS should confirm it.
7. The needs page promises the song within 24 hours of the form being finished, not of the order, since nothing can be made before the answers exist. ClickBank's 24 hour digital delivery rule runs from the order, so this case needs a decision: the page itself may count as delivery, or a reminder email should chase the missing answers.
8. The making page says the words appear first. If lyrics and audio ever land together, the second P and LYRICS_H still read correctly.
9. Buttons: Download The Song 17, Copy Private Link 17, Begin Another Song 18, Tell Us Who It Is For 21, Listen To The Song 18. No exclamation marks anywhere. "Minutes" and "instantly" do not appear; "about a minute" on the needs page describes the form, not delivery.
10. BONUS_P says the three printables are "below". If they sit elsewhere on the page, change that one word.

FEEDBACK SECTION FLAGS
1. What the tick covers. SHARE names her words and the name she typed, nothing else. It does not mention the star rating. If stars will be shown publicly (beside a quote, or as an average), either add "my rating" to the sentence or show stars only for ticked reviews. Please also store the tick with its date and the exact wording she agreed to, and treat a later untick through the EDIT link as a withdrawal: her words come off the site and out of any advertisement.
2. What buyers will write. Honest reviews of a prayer song will often mention an illness, a death, a child's return to the faith or a prayer they feel was answered, and often the loved one's name. Permission from her does not make those lines usable: the bible's no health or outcome claims rule and Meta's policy still apply to a customer quote. TS should decide the screening rule before the first one is published (my suggestion: publish whole and unedited or not at all, never trim a review to change its meaning, and keep any review that mentions a medical detail or an outcome off ads).
3. {name} is left out of the whole block on purpose, so it reads correctly when the song is for herself or for someone who has died. The section is shown to every buyer and asks for honest words, with no reward and no request for a high rating. Keep it that way in the build: do not show the form only to buyers who seem pleased, and do not hide low ratings from the public count if a count is ever shown.
4. Build dependencies in the wording. ERROR says her words are still in the box, so the form must keep its contents when sending fails. THANKS_P ends "you can do that here", so the EDIT link must sit directly beneath it. NAME is written as optional in spirit; decide what shows if she ticks the box but leaves the name empty (suggest making the name required only when the box is ticked).

REVIEWS PAGE FLAGS
1. NAV_TRACK is "My Song" because the limit is two words and "Find My Song" is three. It reads like the familiar "My Order" and says nothing about shipping or about how fast the song is made. If three words are allowed in the header, "Find My Song" is clearer to a first-time visitor who has no song yet; "Song Link" is the two word fallback.
2. "After we have read it" needs a written rule for what reading may hold back. HOW_P is honest only if a permitted review is kept off the page for a stated reason (a medical detail, an outcome claim, a third person's private details, abuse) and never because it is unfavourable. Holding back the unhappy ones while the heading says "What buyers have said" would be selective proof, and US consumer review rules treat suppressing negative reviews as deceptive. TS should decide the rule before the first review is published.
3. Outcomes will arrive in buyers' words (see FEEDBACK SECTION FLAGS 2). This page is on the domain the ads point to and is linked from the sales page header, so a published review saying a prayer was answered or someone recovered becomes an outcome claim on the funnel. Shortening a review to remove that line would break "never edited" and change its meaning, so the choice is publish whole or not at all. If such reviews are held back, add that reason to the rule in flag 2.
4. Build dependencies. "Can be changed or withdrawn at any time" means an untick or an edit on the private page must take the review off this page promptly, and an edited review should be read again before it reappears. Star ratings are collected on the form but must not be rendered here. The EMPTY state must switch to H and P by itself when the first review is published, and SAMPLES should link to the sample section of the sales page only while that section exists. The hidden proof block in 01-landing-page.md says real reviews "will go on this page"; if it is switched back on, point it to the reviews page instead.
