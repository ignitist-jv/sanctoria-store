NOTES

1. Order. The rows follow the three questions in the order a buyer asks them with a card in hand: what am I getting (row 1), when and how does it reach me (row 2), am I safe (row 3). The guarantee sits last so it is the line nearest the payment fields below the banner.

2. Row 1 carries the page's own phrase, "sung by name", so the banner confirms she has landed on the checkout for the thing she just read about. "One original song" tells her it is a single finished song and not a subscription or a library. "Built on Scripture" matches the hero TRUST strip.

3. Row 3 is set like the bracelet banner's guarantee row: "30 day" large, then "full refund." on the first small line and "One-time payment, no subscription." on the second. It reads through as "30 day full refund", the exact wording of the sales page (no hyphen in "30 day", to match the page; "one-time" keeps its hyphen as a compound word).

4. "Nothing is posted" (alt row 2). Sanctoria's other checkouts are for physical goods, so a buyer may expect a parcel. If the ClickBank form for this item shows no shipping fields, the main row 2 is enough. If support starts receiving "where is my package" emails, switch to the alternative.

5. The remake promise appears only in alt row 3. It is the strongest safety line this product has that no competitor banner can copy, but it costs the "one-time payment, no subscription" reassurance, and the sales page states the price terms more often than the remake. Use alt row 3 only if the back end supports a remake request from a reply to the delivery email (open item in note 6 of 01-landing-page.md).

6. "Their first name" (alt row 1) is plural-safe: it reads correctly for one name or two.

7. What is deliberately absent, per the brief and the product bible: no customer count and no "trusted by" (there are no buyers yet), nothing about being blessed, hand-made, or sung, written or prayed over by a person, no promised outcome, no ranking, no use of the competitor's one-word brand, and the word removed from this page by the owner does not appear. Do not carry the bracelet banner's third row over to this product.

8. Word counts. BIG lines: 3, 3, 2 (alternatives 3, 2, 2). SMALL lines: 6, 7, 6 (alternatives 6, 6, 7). All within the limits of 3 and 7.

9. No em dashes or en dashes anywhere. The bracelet banner uses a middle dot before "No questions asked"; none is used here, so the designer may add one before the second small line of row 3 if the layout wants it.
