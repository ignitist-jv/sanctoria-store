INTRO_P: This is nine days of prayer to keep beside the prayer song. Each day asks for about ten minutes.
INTRO_P: Choose a time that can be kept: the kitchen table before the house wakes, or the bedside at night. Read the verse and the short reflection. Pray the prayer, and at the ruled line say or write the name of the one you are praying for. If the song is for your own heart, the name is your own. If the one you are praying for has died, pray it for that soul.
INTRO_P: Then play the song. It is the heart of each day, because it holds the name and the prayer together. Close with the prayers named at the foot of the page.
INTRO_P: If a day is missed, nothing is lost. Pick it up again where it was left.

## DAY: 1
TITLE: Called By Name
VERSE: Fear not, for I have redeemed thee, and called thee by thy name: thou art mine.
REF: Isaias 43:1
VERIFIED: drbo.org, 2026-10-06
REFLECTION: Before this name was ever written down or sung in a chorus, God knew it. He knew it before anyone spoke it aloud at the font. So these nine days do not begin by telling Him something new. They begin by standing before Him and saying the name He already holds, slowly, the way it deserves to be said.
PRAYER: Lord, You call each of us by name, and You do not forget one. Today I bring before You {blank}, this child of Yours, whom You have known from the beginning. I do not come with many words. I come with one name. Receive it, Lord, and receive my prayer with it, for these nine days and beyond them. Amen.
PRACTICE: Play the song once and listen for the name. Then pray one Our Father, one Hail Mary and one Glory Be.

## DAY: 2
TITLE: Leaning On Him
VERSE: Have confidence in the Lord with all thy heart, and lean not upon thy own prudence.
REF: Proverbs 3:5
VERIFIED: drbo.org, 2026-10-06
REFLECTION: Most of us pray with one hand open and the other still holding on. We bring a name to God and then go on carrying it alone in the small hours. Trust is not pretending to feel calm. It is handing over what was never ours to carry by ourselves, and doing it again tomorrow when we find we have picked it back up.
PRAYER: Father, I have carried this name a long way by myself. Today I place {blank} in Your hands, which are larger and gentler than mine. Where I cannot see the way, let me trust that You can. Where I want to manage everything, teach me to lean on You. Give me the faith to leave it with You. Amen.
PRACTICE: Play the song once. Then pray one Our Father, one Hail Mary and one Glory Be.

## DAY: 3
TITLE: Love That Stays
VERSE: Beareth all things, believeth all things, hopeth all things, endureth all things.
REF: 1 Corinthians 13:7
REFLECTION: Saint Paul is describing charity, the love that comes from God. It is not a feeling that rises and falls with the day. It stays. To keep praying over one name, year after year, through good news and through silence, is one of the plainest ways that love bears and hopes and endures. Hardly anyone sees it. God does.
PRAYER: Lord Jesus, teach me to love as You love. Let me hold {blank} before You patiently, without anxiety and without keeping count. When I am tired, let me love by praying. When I have no words left, let the song carry them. Make my heart a quiet place where this name is always welcome. Amen.
PRACTICE: Play the song once. Then pray one Our Father, one Hail Mary and one Glory Be.

## DAY: 4
TITLE: Do Not Grow Weary
VERSE: But they that hope in the Lord shall renew their strength, they shall take wings as eagles, they shall run and not be weary, they shall walk and not faint.
REF: Isaias 40:31
REFLECTION: Prayer that goes on for a long time can feel like standing still. The prophet says otherwise. Those who hope in the Lord are given new strength, not because the waiting is short, but because He meets them in it. Today is the fourth day. Coming back to the page again is itself the hoping.
PRAYER: Lord, renew my strength today. I have prayed for {blank} before, and I will pray again tomorrow. Keep me from growing weary, and from measuring You by what I can see. Let me keep this small appointment with You faithfully, one day at a time, and leave the rest to You. Amen.
PRACTICE: Play the song once. Then pray one Our Father, one Hail Mary and one Glory Be.

## DAY: 5
TITLE: His Peace
VERSE: Peace I leave with you, my peace I give unto you: not as the world giveth, do I give unto you. Let not your heart be troubled, nor let it be afraid.
REF: John 14:27
REFLECTION: Our Lord spoke these words on the night before He died, to friends who were about to be very afraid. The peace He gives is not the world's kind, which depends on everything going well. It is His own, and it can rest in a heart while the questions are still open.
PRAYER: Jesus, giver of peace, let Your peace rest on {blank} today, and on every heart that carries this name. Quiet what is troubled in me. Where I am afraid, stay with me. I do not ask to understand everything. I ask only to be near You, and to leave this name in Your peace. Amen.
PRACTICE: Play the song once, and sit quietly for a minute after it ends. Then pray one Our Father, one Hail Mary and one Glory Be.

## DAY: 6
TITLE: Behold Thy Mother
VERSE: After that, he saith to the disciple: Behold thy mother. And from that hour, the disciple took her to his own.
REF: John 19:27
VERIFIED: drbo.org, 2026-10-06
REFLECTION: From the cross, Our Lord gave His mother to the disciple, and the Church has long seen in that disciple every one of us. Mary knows what it is to stand close to someone she loves and be able to do nothing but stay. A name brought to her is never a burden. She carries it to her Son.
PRAYER: Mary, my Mother, I entrust {blank} to your care today. You stood at the cross and did not leave. Stand with me now. Take this name into your prayers and carry it to your Son, who refuses you nothing that is good. Pray for us, holy Mother of God. Amen.
PRACTICE: Play the song once. Then pray one Our Father, three Hail Marys and one Glory Be.

## DAY: 7
TITLE: Into His Hands
VERSE: Cast thy care upon the Lord, and he shall sustain thee: he shall not suffer the just to waver for ever.
REF: Psalm 54:23
VERIFIED: drbo.org, 2026-10-06
REFLECTION: There is a prayer that asks, and a prayer that lets go. Both are needed. To cast a care upon the Lord is not to stop caring. It is to stop holding on as though everything depended on our own grip. Today the prayer is simple: Thy will be done, said slowly, and meant as far as we are able.
PRAYER: Father, I have told You what is in my heart. Now I give You {blank} without conditions. You love this soul more than anyone on earth is able to. Whatever You will, let it be done, and give me the grace to accept it. Sustain me while I wait. I leave this care with You. Amen.
PRACTICE: Play the song once. Then pray one Our Father, slowly, one Hail Mary and one Glory Be.

## DAY: 8
TITLE: Hope Beyond Sorrow
VERSE: And God shall wipe away all tears from their eyes: and death shall be no more, nor mourning, nor crying, nor sorrow shall be any more, for the former things are passed away.
REF: Apocalypse 21:4
REFLECTION: This is where God is leading His people. Scripture closes with God Himself drying their tears. We are not there yet, and faith does not ask us to pretend that we are. It asks us to hold on to His promise while we wait, and to go on praying for one another along the way.
PRAYER: Lord, You see every tear, mine and those I know nothing about. I place {blank} within Your promise today. Have mercy on us. Keep us in Your grace, and bring us at the last to the place where nothing is lost and no one is missing. Until then, keep my hope alive. Amen.
PRACTICE: Play the song once. Then pray one Our Father, one Hail Mary and one Glory Be.

## DAY: 9
TITLE: Give Thanks
VERSE: Give praise to the Lord, for he is good: for his mercy endureth for ever.
REF: Psalm 117:1
REFLECTION: The ninth day is for thanks. Not because everything has been settled, but because God is good and His mercy does not run out. Give thanks for the life of the one you have prayed for. Give thanks for nine days kept, even imperfectly. A prayer of thanks is never wasted, and it is a fitting way to finish.
PRAYER: Lord, I thank You for the life of {blank}, and for every day You have given. Thank You for hearing me these nine days. Your mercy endures for ever. I do not finish this prayer today. I only set the page down. Keep this name in Your heart always, and keep me faithful in praying it. Amen.
PRACTICE: Play the song once more. Then pray one Our Father, one Hail Mary and one Glory Be, and make the Sign of the Cross.

=== NOTES ===

SCRIPTURE
1. Five of the nine verses come from the verified list and match PS_VERSES in site/prayer-song/api/lyrics.php character for character: 1 Corinthians 13:7 (day 3), Isaias 40:31 (day 4), John 14:27 (day 5), Apocalypse 21:4 (day 8), Psalm 117:1 (day 9). The wording in the brief and in lyrics.php is identical for all eight.
2. Four verses are new and carry VERIFY: yes: Isaias 43:1 (day 1), Proverbs 3:5 (day 2), John 19:27 (day 6), Psalm 54:23 (day 7). I looked each one up on drbo.org on 2026-10-06 and the wording in this file matched what came back. That lookup went through an automated page reader, so please still check them by eye before printing.
3. Day 1 prints only the second half of Isaias 43:1. The full verse begins "And now thus saith the Lord that created thee, O Jacob, and formed thee, O Israel:" and then continues with the words used here. If a partial verse is not acceptable, the reference could be printed as "from Isaias 43:1", or the whole verse used (it adds 16 words to the page).
4. Jeremias 30:17, Psalm 90:11 and Luke 15:20 were left out on purpose. Each is tied to one intention and reads wrongly on a page prayed for someone who has died or in thanksgiving.
5. No other Scripture is quoted. Two phrases inside prayers echo Scripture without quoting it: "Thy will be done" (day 7 reflection, from the Our Father) and "Your mercy endures for ever" (day 9 prayer, modernised from the day's own verse). Change the second to "endureth" if the echo should match the verse exactly.

ONE DOCUMENT FOR EVERY CASE
6. Every day page was checked against all eight intentions and against the three kinds of person: someone living, someone who has died, and the buyer herself. No page says the person is ill, alive, absent, young, male or female, and no pronoun is used for them. The person is called "this child of Yours", "this soul" or "this name", which read correctly in all three cases.
7. The intro page carries the two instructions that make this work: if the song is for her own heart the name is her own, and if the person has died she prays it for that soul. Those two sentences should not be cut for space.
8. Day 8 asks God to "bring us at the last to the place where nothing is lost and no one is missing". It is a petition and a hope. It does not say anyone is in heaven.
9. Day 9 thanks God "for the life of {blank}", which holds for the living, the dead and herself.

CATHOLIC ACCURACY
10. The companion calls itself "nine days of prayer" throughout and never a novena. The landing page and the product bible describe this bonus as "a short novena". That is ordinary usage and I do not think it is wrong, but the document itself makes no claim to be an approved or indulgenced novena, and the sales copy should not add one.
11. Day 6 says the Church "has long seen in that disciple every one of us". That is the standard reading of John 19:27 (Catechism 2679 and 968 to 970 speak of Mary's motherhood of the faithful). It also says her Son "refuses you nothing that is good", which is devotional language in the Cana tradition, not a promise about this prayer. Cut that clause if it reads as too strong.
12. The Our Father, Hail Mary and Glory Be are named only. No text of any traditional prayer is printed. The memorial card uses the traditional terms "eternal rest" and "perpetual light" inside an original prayer and does not reproduce the Eternal Rest prayer itself.
13. Nothing says or implies that the song, the pages or the card were blessed, and nothing promises an outcome. The closing page says so in plain words.

LENGTH
14. Day pages run from 148 to 168 words in total (title, verse, reference, reflection, prayer, practice), measured by script. Days 4, 5 and 8 carry the longest verses, so their reflections are the shortest. If any page overflows in 11.5pt type, trim the reflection, not the prayer.
15. The intro is 139 words and the closing 98, both inside the limits.

THE CARD
16. Three variants, as corrected: PRAYER for someone living, PRAYER_MEMORIAL whenever the "who" answer is someone who has passed (whatever the intention), PRAYER_SELF and HEADING_SELF when she is praying for herself. The memorial card uses the ordinary HEADING, "A Daily Prayer For {name}", which reads correctly for someone who has died.
17. PRAYER uses {name} three times in a row with no pronoun, because the card cannot know he or she without the derived pronoun token. If the builder has reliable pronouns, the second and third {name} could become "him" or "her"; as written it is safe without them.
18. PRAYER_SELF uses {name} once ("You know me by my name, {name}"). If that reads oddly with a given name, the clause can be cut to "You know me by name" with no other change.
19. The code prints the intention's verse beneath the card prayer. For someone who has passed, the verse will still be the one for her chosen intention (for example Jeremias 30:17 if she chose healing for a person who has died). That pairing is a build decision: I would print Apocalypse 21:4 under every memorial card regardless of intention.
