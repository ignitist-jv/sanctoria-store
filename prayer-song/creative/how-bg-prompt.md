**Slug:** `how-bg`  |  **Model:** `gpt_image_2_5`  |  **Aspect ratio:** 16:9 landscape  |  **Alt text:** empty (decoration)

**Idea:** An empty choir loft at dusk, seen straight on: two towers of organ pipes left and right, a dark recess between them, one small amber window above, two candles and an open hymn book. It gives "church choir and organ" as a place waiting for music, with no singer, organist or any person, so the section never implies who performs the song.

## How it is composed for the page

- Centre of the frame (where the heading, three steps and button sit) is the darkest, plainest area. Brightness lives in the two pipe towers, the two candle pools and the small window at top centre.
- Wide desktop band: nothing essential in the top or bottom tenth, so trimming top and bottom is safe.
- Phone strip (middle third, full height): still shows the window, the dim central pipes and the innermost bright pipes of each tower on its two sides.
- Built from big simple shapes (upright pipes, one arch, one round glow) so it survives the 70 to 80 percent brown veil. Page browns for reference: #2B1E19 to #3A2A22; highlights toward bronze #9A7B4F. Hex values are kept out of the prompt on purpose so the model cannot print them.

## If it comes back wrong

- **A person, silhouette or statue appears, or someone sits at the organ:** reject. This is the one fixed rule. Regenerate; if it repeats, delete the choir stalls sentence, since empty seats tempt the model to fill them.
- **Readable text (hymn board numbers, stop labels, notes on the page):** reject, or regenerate with the music stand sentence removed. The hymn book is the likeliest source.
- **Too bright or too contrasty for cream text:** do not regenerate first. Raise the page veil toward 80 percent. Only regenerate if the centre itself has a bright object in it.
- **Centre is busy (bright central pipes, a console with keyboards, a big window):** regenerate. The middle third must stay dark from one quarter down to the bottom.
- **Phone crop looks like a plain brown wall:** the towers were drawn too far apart. Regenerate, or shift the mobile crop a little and scale the image so one tower edge enters the strip.
- **Light rays, haze or a glowing window like a spotlight:** reject. It reads as "light from heaven", which the brief forbids.
- **Cold, grey, ruined or spooky:** reject. It should feel like a cared for parish church after evening prayer.
- **Looks painted or rendered, or pipes are warped and uneven:** regenerate. Pipes should be straight, evenly spaced and plainly metal.
- **Window shows a saint or a face:** regenerate. Plain amber petal glass only.
