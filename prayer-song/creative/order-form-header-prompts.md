**Job of this image:** reassurance at the moment of paying. Same cream, same warmth, same brand as the page she just left. It is not an ad and it must sit quietly above the payment fields.

**Nothing is generated as text.** The Sanctoria logo and the words "Your Prayer Song" are composited on afterwards. Hex values are kept out of both prompts on purpose, because this model renders text well and will print anything that looks like a code.

---

## Ratio and crop (applies to both)

`gpt_image_2_5` does not offer 4:1. Its ratios are 1:1, 3:2, 2:3, 4:3, 3:4, 16:9, 9:16, 21:9, 27:16, 16:27, 9:8, 8:9, 4:5 and 5:4. **Generate at 21:9**, the widest it has, at 2k so the cropped strip is still well over 1600 pixels wide.

- **Never trim the sides.** Keep the full width.
- **For 4:1:** keep the central horizontal band whose height is the image width divided by 4. That means trimming 20.8 percent of the height off the top and 20.8 percent off the bottom (keep rows from 20.8 to 79.2 percent).
- **For 5:1:** trim 26.7 percent off the top and off the bottom (keep rows from 26.7 to 73.3 percent).
- Worked example: if the file comes back 2352 x 1008, the 4:1 band is 2352 x 588, from y = 210 to y = 798. The 5:1 band is 2352 x 470, from y = 269 to y = 739.
- Then resize to whatever the ClickBank template asks for.

Both prompts are written against the 21:9 frame the model actually draws. Everything that matters sits between 30 and 70 percent of that frame's height, which is inside the 5:1 band, so it is also inside the middle 80 percent of the 4:1 band.

## Where the logo and the words go (applies to both)

Measured on the FINAL cropped banner:

- **Clean zone:** the left 50 percent of the width, full height. Bare cream linen, near flat in tone. This is 5 points wider than the 45 percent in the brief, on purpose: the logo lockup and "Your Prayer Song" on one line need about 45 percent of the width between them, and the extra 5 gives them air on the right.
- **Logo** (`site/images/logo.png`, the gold cross and dark "Sanctoria"): left edge at 4 percent of the width, about 24 percent wide, vertically centred. On a 1600 x 400 banner that is roughly 330 x 150.
- **Optional hairline:** a thin bronze #9A7B4F vertical rule at 26 percent, about 40 percent of the banner height.
- **"Your Prayer Song":** one line, Fraunces, deep brown #2B1E19, from 28 percent to about 49 percent of the width, vertically centred on the logo. About 40 pixels cap to baseline area on a 1600 x 400 banner.
- **Picture interest:** 56 to 96 percent of the width. 50 to 56 is a quiet buffer.
- If the template shrinks the banner on phones, the lockup still reads because nothing is behind it. If the template instead crops to the centre on phones, ask for a second, narrower export rather than re-centring this one.

### If it comes back wrong

- **Any person, hand or shadow of a person:** reject. Fixed rule.
- **Readable words, notes or staves on the pages, or a title on the cover:** the likeliest failure. Regenerate once. If it repeats, change "an open hymn book" to "a closed, plain, cloth bound hymn book with a blank cover" and delete the two sentences about the pages.
- **Something in the left half (a fold, a shadow, a second sprig, a cup):** regenerate. A faint tone change can be fixed by laying a cream #F7F3EC gradient from the left edge fading out at 52 percent; an object cannot.
- **A table edge, wall or window appears:** regenerate. A horizontal line behind the logo is the thing this layout exists to avoid.
- **Objects too tall, flame or book touching the top or bottom quarter:** check against the 5:1 band. If the flame tip is cut at 4:1, regenerate; do not shift the crop up, the banner will look bottom heavy.
- **Cream has gone grey, cool or yellow:** sample the left half. It should sit close to #F7F3EC. A slight warm correction is fine; a grey render looks dirty beside the white order form, so regenerate.
- **A cross, rosary or prayer card has been added:** regenerate. One devotional object only.
- **Candle glows like a halo, or light rays appear:** reject.
- **Looks like a spa or skincare advert:** the olive sprig is the cause. Delete the olive sentences and the two mentions of the sprig; the book and candle hold the frame alone.
- **Too empty once cropped:** that is the intended register for a checkout header. Add the logo and words before judging it.

### If it comes back wrong

- **Any text, ring of small print or logo on the label:** the likeliest failure. Reject and regenerate; if it repeats, use the composite option above.
- **A sleeve, turntable, tonearm or box appears:** reject. That is the "it comes in the post" reading.
- **Record drawn as an ellipse or tilted:** regenerate. It must be a true circle from straight above.
- **Record too black and heavy, banner feels dark:** lower its weight in an editor by lifting the blacks toward deep brown #2B1E19, or regenerate. If it still dominates the form, switch to the recommended prompt.
- **Record too large or centred, crossing left of 60 percent:** regenerate. The left half must stay bare.
- **Hard white glare wedge or rainbow sheen on the grooves:** regenerate.
- **Label not fully inside the 5:1 band:** regenerate; a cut label looks like a mistake.
- **Any person, hand, cross, candle or extra object:** reject.

- **Logo:** `site/images/logo.png` has a dark "Sanctoria" wordmark made for cream pages and will disappear on this ground. Use an all gold or gold and cream version, as on the bracelet banner.
- **Insurance for the fade:** sample the ground colour at about 20 percent of the width, fill a layer with it, and mask it from solid at 52 percent to clear at 68 percent, placed over the image and under the text. This guarantees an even left side even if the model leaves a faint lighter patch. It is the same treatment the bracelet banner has.
- **Even ground:** if the left side shows blotches or banding, flatten it with the same sampled colour from 0 to 50 percent. Add 1 to 2 percent of fine noise afterwards so the flat fill and the photograph share one texture and the gradient does not band when the file is compressed.
- **Match the family:** put this banner beside the bracelet one and compare the left ground. Nudge hue and darkness until the two blacks match; a cooler or greyer black here will look like a different brand.
- **Row wording:** one row should state "digital song, delivered by email" in the copywriter's words. All claims in the rows must match the sales page.
- **Phone check:** view the finished banner at 375 pixels wide. If the three rows are too small to read, that is a text size decision, not an image fault; the record still reads at that size.

### If it comes back wrong

- **Any person, hand, silhouette or reflection of a person:** reject. Fixed rule.
- **Text, a ring of small print or a logo on the record label:** the likeliest failure. Zoom to 200 percent. Reject and regenerate; if it repeats, generate with the label sentences cut down to "a plain blank terracotta label" or composite the approved page record from `prayer-song/creative/vinyl/`.
- **A hard vertical edge, a panel, or a visible box around the picture:** regenerate once. If the picture itself is good, keep it and use the masked ground layer from the compositing notes to make the fade.
- **A vignette ring or oval pool of light with a visible rim:** regenerate. The falloff must have no shape.
- **Left side not dark or not even (grey haze, a lighter band, stone visible left of 55 percent, a horizon line):** a faint unevenness is fixed by the flat fill. A visible object, line or table edge on the left means regenerate.
- **The record sits too far left, crossing 60 percent while still lit:** regenerate. Gold text over lit grooves will not read.
- **A sleeve, turntable, tonearm, box or second object appears:** reject. That is the "it comes in the post" reading.
- **Record warped, lumpy or drawn as a circle instead of a flat oval:** regenerate. If it keeps failing, change "about 30 degrees above the surface" to "about 40 degrees above the surface".
- **Hard white glare wedge or rainbow sheen on the grooves:** regenerate.
- **Candle has a halo, a glowing disc, rays or smoke:** reject. The flame is a small plain teardrop.
- **A cross, rosary, beads or a bracelet has been added:** reject. One devotional object only, and beads would look like the item for sale.
- **Flame tip or the bottom of the record is cut by the 3:1 band:** regenerate. Do not slide the crop; the band is central by design.
- **Stone has gone grey, cold or polished like a kitchen worktop:** regenerate. It should be matte honey beige, close to the stone in the bracelet banner.
- **Whole image too bright, record on a fully lit tabletop:** regenerate. If it repeats, add "deliberately underexposed" to the first sentence of LIGHT AND TONE.
- **Brightest area is not between 80 and 90 percent of the width:** acceptable if it is anywhere right of 75 percent. Left of that, regenerate.
- **Looks gloomy or funereal once the text is on:** lift the right side only, by about a third of a stop, and warm it slightly. Do not lift the left ground.
