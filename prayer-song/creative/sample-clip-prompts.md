# Prayer Song: sample card clip prompts

Three silent looping clips, one above the play button of each sample card. 5 seconds, portrait 3:4, muted. Machine readable copy: `sample-clip-prompts.json` (same prompts, same order).

These clips illustrate the kind of moment a song is made for. They are not customers and not testimonials. Nobody speaks, nobody looks at the lens, there is no text in frame and nothing shows an outcome. The page already says the songs are samples made by Sanctoria; keep that line next to the cards.

## Pipeline

1. **Step A, the still.** Model `gpt_image_2_5`, aspect 3:4. Paste the still prompt whole. This image becomes the first frame, so approve it carefully: whatever is wrong in the still stays wrong for 5 seconds.
2. **Step B, the motion.** Model `seedance_2_5`, image to video, start image = the approved still, 5 seconds, 3:4, audio off. Paste the motion prompt whole.

Notes:
- The image model takes no negative prompt, so every exclusion is written into the prompt itself. Do not trim the LOOK or WHAT THIS IS NOT blocks; they are identical in all three so the set matches.
- This look is deliberately different from the ten tile photographs on the same page. The tiles use a shallow depth of field and a warm grade. These clips use deep focus, flat contrast and plain colour, because the owner asked for a believable phone video and a blurred background is the first thing that makes a clip look like an advertisement.
- Sizes are given as ratios to things in the frame (a head is one sixth of the frame height, the phone is as long as a hand), never in real units, per LEARNINGS.md.
- Each scene has exactly one clearly visible hand, and no phone screen faces the camera. Both choices are there to remove the two most common failures before they happen.
- The loop: each motion prompt asks the clip to end where it began. If the video tool offers an end frame setting, set it to the same still as the start frame. If the loop still jumps, a short crossfade of the last few frames into the first at build time hides it.
- The approved still can double as the poster image shown before the video loads.
- Check each still at the real card width before running Step B. If the two faces do not read at that size, regenerate with "each head is about one fifth of the frame height".

## Checklist for approving a still

- No eyes toward the lens, every mouth closed.
- No letters, numbers, icons or interface anywhere, including on mugs, magnets, book spines and the phone.
- The phone shows its blank back only.
- One visible hand, five fingers.
- Background sharp, not blurred. Plain colour, no golden glow.
- One Catholic cue, small, in the background.
- Clip 3: the child's face is not visible.

## 1. The son: "Lead Daniel Home"

**Slug:** `son`

**Sample card:** For a son. A return to the faith. Gentle guitar.

**The idea:** An ordinary weekday visit at the kitchen table, not a reconciliation scene. The gesture is the one every mother has: her hand on his forearm, two soft pats. The brief suggested a hand placed over a hand. I moved it to her hand on his sleeve, already resting there in the first frame, because stacked hands are where finger errors come from and because a hand that travels across the table cannot return for the loop. His hands are hidden around a mug, so there is exactly one hand to get right. The phone is propped against the sugar bowl facing them, so the camera sees its blank back and there is no screen to render.

**Alt text:** A mother and her grown son listening together at the kitchen table

**Still prompt (Step A, `gpt_image_2_5`, 3:4):**

```
SCENE: An ordinary family kitchen on a weekday afternoon. A Hispanic American woman about 64 and her adult son about 34 sit at the corner of a worn wooden kitchen table, on adjacent sides, so their shoulders are close. She is on the left of the frame: dark hair streaked with grey and pulled back in a clip, a soft lined face, reading glasses hanging on a cord at her chest, a plain lilac cardigan over a cotton blouse. He is on the right: short dark hair, a day of stubble, a plain grey work sweatshirt with the sleeves pushed up to the elbow. Both of his hands are wrapped around a plain white mug, so most of his fingers are hidden behind it. Her right hand rests flat on his forearm just above the wrist, palm down, five fingers together and relaxed. Her other hand is in her lap, out of sight. She is looking at the side of his face with a small, tender, closed mouth smile. He is looking down at the table in front of him, listening, the corners of his closed mouth just lifted. It is an everyday visit, calm and unremarkable. On the table between them a phone stands propped against a ceramic sugar bowl, facing the two of them, so the camera sees only its plain dark back in a matte case with no logo and no markings. The phone is about as long as her hand from wrist to fingertip. Also on the table: a second plain mug, a folded dish towel, a fruit bowl with two bananas. Behind them the kitchen is fully in focus: a window over the sink at the left edge of the frame, partly cut off by the frame, a dish rack with a few plates, a roll of paper towels, a refrigerator with a few plain coloured magnets and nothing pinned to it. High on the wall beside the window hangs one small plain wooden crucifix, about half the height of the son's head as drawn, unlit and unremarkable, never the focus. CAMERA POSITION: filmed by someone sitting at the far end of the same table, about four feet away, phone held upright at chest height. Both people are seen from the waist up across the table top. Each head is about one sixth of the frame height and sits in the upper half of the frame. The near edge of the table and the top rail of an empty chair cut into the bottom of the frame. LIGHT: soft grey daylight from the window on the left falling across the table and the sides of their faces, the ceiling light off, shadows open and a little grainy. LOOK: A single frame taken from an ordinary home video, filmed on a phone held upright by a family member in the same room. It must look like amateur family footage, not a professional photograph and not an advertisement. Phone camera character: a slightly wide lens and deep focus, so the people, the furniture and the back wall are all acceptably sharp at once, with no blurred background and no bokeh. Slightly flat contrast, ordinary automatic exposure, the brightest part of the window or lamp a little blown out, fine sensor noise visible in the shadows, plain and slightly muted colour with the mixed white balance of a real room. Available light only, exactly as described above. No flash, no studio light, no rim light, no glow, no colour grade, no film look. Framing is imperfect the way a real handheld clip is: the picture tilted very slightly off level, the people a little off centre, something ordinary cutting into one edge of the frame. Real people, not models: natural skin with pores, lines, age spots and uneven tone, unstyled hair, ordinary slightly worn clothes with no printing on them, a lived in home with everyday clutter. Anatomy is natural and correct. Every visible hand has five fingers with natural knuckles and nails. Portrait orientation, aspect ratio 3:4, full frame. WHAT THIS IS NOT: Nobody looks at the camera or is aware of it. Nobody speaks: every mouth is closed and relaxed. Nobody poses, holds anything up, points, waves or gives a thumbs up. The people are absorbed in each other and in listening. Quiet emotion only, with no tears on any face. There is no text anywhere in the frame: no captions, no subtitles, no usernames, no social media interface, no record indicator, no timestamp, no battery or signal icons, no watermark, no logo, no star rating, no border, no black bars, and no writing on any object, packaging, magnet, mug, book spine, picture or clothing. The image is the raw camera picture only. It is not a screenshot, and no phone or hand is held up in the foreground. The only phone in the scene is the one described, and its screen cannot be seen from where the camera is. No priest, no nun, no church, no hospital, no medical equipment, no pills, no wheelchair, no walking frame, no sick bed. No halos, no light rays, no glowing figures. Not cinematic, not an illustration, not a painting, not a 3D render.
```

**Motion prompt (Step B, `seedance_2_5`, 5 seconds, 3:4, no audio):**

```

It barely moves: only a faint natural sway and breathing drift, never more than one fiftieth of the frame width, with the framing otherwise unchanged. ACTION: Both people breathe gently. The mother's hand on her son's forearm gives two slow, soft pats and then rests flat again exactly where it started. As she pats, his eyes drop to her hand, his closed mouth lifts into a slightly fuller smile, and he gives one small nod. Then his gaze returns to the table where it began. She blinks once and keeps looking at the side of his face. His hands stay around the mug the whole time. The phone propped against the sugar bowl, the mugs and everything else on the table stay perfectly still. MUST NOT HAPPEN: Nobody opens their mouth, speaks, sings, mouths words or laughs aloud. Lips stay closed from the first frame to the last. Nobody looks at the camera or turns toward it. No new person, hand, object or animal enters the frame. Nothing in the room appears, disappears, moves or changes. The phone in the scene does not move, light up or show its screen. No text, captions, subtitles, icons, logos or watermark appear at any point. No zoom, no push in, no pull back, no pan, no orbit, no refocus. No scene change, no cut, no fade, no change of light or colour, no slow motion, no speed change, no added background blur. Hands keep five fingers and their natural shape throughout. Faces keep the same identity and the same age as in the first frame. LOOP: by the last frame every person and the camera have settled back to exactly where they were in the first frame, so the clip repeats without a visible jump. Silent, no audio.
```

## 2. The mother: "Lay Your Hand On Siobhan"

The mother is dressed, upright, hair set, earrings in: well and at home. The gesture is the daughter's head tipping to rest against her mother's and easing back. The mother's hands are under the blanket and the daughter shows one hand only, on her mother's sleeve. The rosary on the side table is the single Catholic cue.

## If a generation comes back wrong

Add the one line to the end of the named prompt and run it again. Fix the still first; a motion prompt cannot repair a bad first frame.

| What went wrong | Where to fix | The one line to add |
|---|---|---|
| Someone talks, mouths words or glances at the camera | Motion prompt | `Lips stay pressed closed and perfectly still for all 5 seconds, and every pair of eyes stays lowered, never toward the camera.` |
| A lit or readable phone screen, or an interface drawn over the picture (record dot, timestamp, caption) | Still prompt | `The phone shows only its blank back, no screen is visible anywhere, and nothing is drawn on top of the picture: it is the raw camera image with no interface.` |
| Extra, fused or missing fingers | Still prompt | `Only one hand is visible in the whole frame, resting flat with five clearly separate fingers; every other hand is hidden.` |
| The child's face is visible or too prominent (clip 3) | Still prompt | `The child's face is pressed into her neck and cannot be seen at all; the camera sees only the back of the child's head.` |
| It looks like an advertisement: blurred background, golden glow, smooth gliding camera | Still prompt for the look, motion prompt for the camera | `Everything from the foreground to the back wall is equally sharp, in flat, plain, slightly dull phone footage, with the camera almost motionless in an unsteady hand.` |

If the mother in clip 2 reads as unwell, add to the still prompt: `She is healthy, upright and bright eyed, dressed for visitors, in her own living room on an ordinary afternoon.`
