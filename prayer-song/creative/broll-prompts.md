# Prayer Song ad b-roll, scene prompts

The renders are gitignored. These prompts are the source of truth: anything in
`output/ads/2026-10-08-prayer-song-batch1/assets/broll/` can be rebuilt from here.

Model: `nano_banana_pro` at 2k, 4:5 for the stills. `seedance_2_5` in
`omni_reference` mode, 3:4, 5 seconds, for the clips, with the still as
`start_image`. Each clip must loop: the last frame returns to the first, so a
scene can repeat in the edit without a visible jump.

House rules that apply to every scene: amateur family phone footage, never an
advertisement. Deep focus, no bokeh, available light, visible sensor grain.
Nobody looks at the camera. No open mouths, nobody speaks or sings. No text
anywhere in frame and no visible phone screen. A small crucifix or rosary
somewhere, never the focus. The lower sixth of the frame stays calm and low in
detail, because captions are composited there. Sizes are anchored to in-frame
ratios, never to millimetres.

## 01 mother and daughter, sofa  (REPLACED 2026-10-10)

The first version had the two sharing WIRED earbuds. The cord ran down, met in
a Y and ended in mid air, with nothing plugged into the face-down phone on her
lap. Cable geometry is this model's known failure mode, so this version has no
cable at all: wireless buds, one each, with the open charging case on the
cushion between them carrying the "same source" cue instead. A bud or case that
renders imperfectly is just a small white object on a sofa. A broken cord is
the defect itself.
