# Prayer Song: playbook checklist

Shape: `QUIZ` + `PERSONAL`. ClickBank. $39 front end. No persona.

Flow: landing page with the pitch, what they get and the bonuses, then a chat box that asks who the
song is for and what it is for, then the opt in to send their song, then checkout.
Reference: prayersong.com. Full flow in PRODUCT-FLOW.md. Playbook: ~/projects/product-factory/PRODUCTION-PLAYBOOK.md
