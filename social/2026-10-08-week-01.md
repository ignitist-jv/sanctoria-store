## Compliance

- **No em-dashes or en-dashes.** Hyphens only in compounds.
- **No banned vocabulary.** Register: mercy, blessing, peace, rest, grace, guard, carry, devotion.
- **Zero fabricated proof.** No counts, ratings, testimonials or scarcity. Post 6 gives no example names at all, deliberately.
- **Three liturgical claims only, all safe:** October as the Month of the Holy Rosary, Monday as the Joyful Mysteries, Saturday as a Marian day. "Our Lady of the Rosary" is used as a title, never as a feast falling today. **No saint is assigned to any date.**
- **No intercession claim.** Every prayer is written for her to say. Nowhere does Sanctoria state or imply that it prays for anyone by name, and no post says the names are collected or taken anywhere.
- **No outcome promised.** Every line is a petition, never a result.
- **Product claims on post 7 only**, and only the three the brand owns.
- **No Prayer Song.** It cannot be bought yet.
- **No emoji anywhere**, a change from the previous draft. The three measured pages carry none, and an emoji on a prayer is decoration on something that should not be decorated.

## Written and cut

1. **13 October as the Fatima anniversary.** The final apparition was 13 October 1917, the strongest Tuesday hook available. Cut: the universal feast of Our Lady of Fatima is 13 May, so a devotional reader could fairly read it as a feast-day claim on the wrong date.
2. **"We will pray for every name below."** The line that would most raise post 6's comment count. It is false, and it would be told to the audience least able to forgive it.
3. **"And let the month be gentler than we are afraid of"** on post 1. Reads beautifully, and it is a promise about an outcome dressed as a petition.
4. **A prayer naming an illness.** The strongest emotional post available and the most shareable. Cut because a healing prayer invites healing claims in the comments underneath a page that sells a devotional object, and that is a thread you cannot moderate your way out of.
5. **A saint-of-the-day post.** The October date assignments cannot be verified to a standard that survives a Catholic reader.
6. **Example names on post 6.** The previous draft used "My son Michael" and "Teresa, my sister" as prompts. Two invented names at the top of a thread meant to fill with real ones is the one place on this page where fabrication would actually be noticed.
7. **"Say this and watch what happens by Sunday."** The highest-engagement device on pages of this kind. Banned on the outcome rule, and on taste.
