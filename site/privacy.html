Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sanctoria collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you visit sanctoriastore.com and when you order the St Benedict Marian Bracelet. We keep this simple and honest, because the families who trust us deserve nothing less. By using our site, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

We collect only what we need to serve you well:

  • Order information. When you place an order, our retailer ClickBank collects the details needed to process your payment and fulfill your order, including your name, shipping address, billing information, and the items you selected. Payment card details are handled by ClickBank and its processors; Sanctoria does not see or store your full card number.
  • Contact information. If you email us or sign up for updates, we collect your email address and any information you choose to include in your message.
  • Browsing and analytics data. When you visit the site, we automatically receive standard technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, and the pages you view. This helps us understand how the site is used and keep it working properly.
  • Cookies and pixels. We use cookies and similar technologies, including the Meta (Facebook) pixel and web analytics tools, to measure site activity and advertising performance. See the section on cookies and tracking technologies below.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process, fulfill, and deliver your order, and keep you informed about its status.
  • Provide customer support and respond to your questions and requests.
  • Send you email updates about your order, new blessings, and offers, when you have opted in. You can opt out at any time.
  • Measure and improve our advertising, including understanding which ads lead to visits and orders.
  • Protect against fraud, secure our site, and meet our legal obligations.

Who We Share Information With

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the partners who help us run our business, and only as needed:

  • Payment processor. ClickBank is the retailer and payment processor for orders placed through our site. They handle billing, receipts, and refunds under their own privacy practices.
  • Shipping carriers. We share your name and address with the carriers who deliver your bracelet.
  • Service providers. We use trusted providers for email, analytics, advertising measurement, and site hosting. They may process information on our behalf under confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal reasons. We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and property of Sanctoria, our customers, or others.

We never sell your personal information to third parties.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We use them, along with pixels and analytics tags, to remember your preferences, understand site traffic, and measure the performance of our advertising, including through the Meta pixel and analytics tools. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. If you disable cookies, some parts of the site may not work as intended. You may also adjust ad personalization settings through the platforms that serve you ads, such as Meta.

Your Email Choices and Unsubscribe

If you receive marketing emails from us, every message includes an unsubscribe link. You can opt out at any time, and we will honor your request promptly. We may still send you order-related messages, such as shipping confirmations, because they are necessary to complete your order.

Data Retention

We keep your information only as long as it is needed for the purposes described in this policy, including fulfilling your order, providing support, honoring the 30-Day Promise, and meeting legal, tax, and accounting requirements. When information is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify it.

Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information against loss, misuse, and unauthorized access. Payment processing is handled by ClickBank over secure, encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect your information with care.

Children

Our site and products are intended for adults. They are not directed to children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of the personal information we hold about you, and to ask questions about how we use it. To make a request, email us at support@sanctoriastore.com. We will respond within a reasonable time and may need to verify your identity before acting. For payment and order records held by ClickBank, requests may also be directed to ClickBank as the retailer of record.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date above. Significant changes will be reflected on this page, so we encourage you to review it periodically.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, please email us at support@sanctoriastore.com. We answer every message within one business day.
