How To Use Your Gifts

Print what you can. Pray what you read.

The Prayer Card is meant to live beside your bracelet. Print it on heavy paper, fold it, or laminate it. A small lamp and a quiet minute at sunrise is all you need to begin.

The Workbook is best worked through slowly, a page or two at a time. Keep a pen nearby and let it guide your prayers for the family you love.

The 7-Day Devotional is lovely to read on paper. Print it, or keep it on your tablet, and walk through one day each morning with your bracelet and a journal.

The 30-Day Novena is long, so you do not need to print it all. Print the week ahead, or open it on your phone each morning. One day at a time.

If you miss a day, simply pick up where you left off. The grace is in the returning.
