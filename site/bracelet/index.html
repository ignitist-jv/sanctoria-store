Why The Saint Benedict Medal

A Prayer Of Protection 1,500 Years Old

The letters on the medal are not decoration. They are a 1,500-year-old Latin prayer of protection: Vade Retro Satana, "Begone Satan," and Crux Sacra Sit Mihi Lux, "May the Holy Cross be my light." The Church has long blessed this medal for the protection of the home, the body, and the family.

Is it really blessed, and by whom?
Yes. Every bracelet is hand-blessed by Benedictine monks at the abbey before it leaves our hands. It ships to you already blessed and ready to wear or to give.
How long until it arrives?
We ship within 24 hours of your order. Most families in the US receive theirs in 5 to 7 days, with free shipping.
Will it fit me?
Yes. The adjustable chain extender lets the bracelet size up or down, so it fits most wrists comfortably, whether slender or fuller.

It is real. Genuine ebony wood beads, an antique bronze Saint Benedict medal, and a bronze crucifix charm. Nothing plastic, nothing cheap.
