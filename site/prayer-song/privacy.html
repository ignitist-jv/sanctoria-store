Privacy Policy for Your Prayer Song

Effective date: October 16, 2026

This policy explains what we collect when you fill in the form for Your Prayer Song and place an order, how we use it, and who we share it with. It applies to the Your Prayer Song pages at sanctoriastore.com.

What we collect

  • Your answers in the form. Who the prayer is for, that person's first name and, if you give it, how the name is said; what the prayer is for; the musical style and voice you choose; and any short note you add.
  • Your contact details. Your email address and your first name, so that we can send you your song.
  • Order information. Our payment processor Stripe collects the details needed to take your payment, such as your card details and billing information. We receive a record of the order, including your email address, the order number and the item bought. Your card details go to Stripe and are never seen or stored by Sanctoria.
  • Your song. The lyrics written for you, the finished recording, and the printable pages made from your order.
  • Your review, if you leave one. The stars, your words, the name you ask us to show, and whether you gave permission for it to be shared.
  • Messages. Anything you write to us by email.
  • Technical and advertising data. Standard information such as your IP address, browser and device type, the page that referred you, and which of our advertisements you came from.

About the person you name

A prayer song names someone other than you. Please give a first name only. We ask you not to include a surname, an address, a date of birth, a diagnosis or other sensitive details in the note. We use the name and the note only to write and record the song, to check that the name is sung, and to make the printable pages.

How we use this information

  • To write, record, check and deliver your song and the printable pages.
  • To remake the song if the name is not sung right, and to answer your questions.
  • To send you the emails about your order. If you begin the form and give your email address but do not finish, we may send you a reminder about completing it.
  • To send you news and offers from Sanctoria, which you can stop at any time with the unsubscribe link in every email.
  • To measure and improve our advertising, and to protect against fraud and misuse.

Who helps us, and what they receive

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the companies that help us provide Your Prayer Song, and only as far as each one needs:

  • Stripe, the payment processor for your order, which receives your payment details in order to take the payment. Stripe's own privacy policy, at stripe.com/privacy, explains how it handles payment data.
  • An artificial intelligence writing service (Anthropic), which receives your answers in order to write the lyrics.
  • An artificial intelligence music and voice service (ElevenLabs), which receives the lyrics, including the first name, in order to record the song and to check it.
  • Our email service (Kit), which receives your email address, your first name, the title of your song, the first name it is for, what the prayer is for, and your private link, in order to send your emails.
  • Our website host (Liquid Web), where your order, your answers and your song are stored.
  • Advertising and analytics services (ClickMagick and Meta), which receive information about visits and orders so that we can measure our advertising. They do not receive your answers, your note or your song.
  • Authorities or advisers, where the law requires it or where it is needed to protect the rights and safety of Sanctoria, our customers or others.

Your private link

Your song is kept on a private page with a long, random web address. The address is not listed anywhere and search engines are told not to index it. It is not protected by a password, so anyone who has the address can listen and download. Share it only with people you want to have it.

Cookies

We use a small number of cookies and similar technologies to remember your answers while you move from the form to the checkout, and to measure which advertisements lead to visits and orders. You can block cookies in your browser settings. The form may not carry your answers through to your order if you do.

How long we keep it

We keep your order, your answers and your song for as long as your private page is available and as long as we need them to support your order, honour the 30 day refund, and meet legal, tax and accounting requirements. Answers given in a form that was never followed by an order are kept no longer than we need them, and you can ask us to delete them at any time.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to send you a copy of what we hold about your order, to correct it, or to delete your song, your answers and your private page. Depending on where you live you may have further rights under your local law. To make a request, email support@sanctoriastore.com from the address you ordered with. Deleting a song is permanent, and the link will stop working.

Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. Payments are processed by Stripe over encrypted connections, and your card details are never seen or stored by Sanctoria. No method of sending or storing information is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

Your Prayer Song is intended for adults. It is not directed to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. A song may be a prayer for a child, in which case we ask for the child's first name only.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@sanctoriastore.com. We answer every message within one business day.
