## COMPLIANCE CHECK

- **Personal attributes:** no option or line asserts the reader's religion, health, finances or family trouble. Every option is framed as what she has in mind or what she wants the object to do. Q1 option 3 is "Someone I pray for by name", which states her prayer, not anyone's circumstance. No illness, diagnosis, addiction or marital breakdown anywhere, including by implication. The only hardship-adjacent line is "the moment the news lands", which names a moment without naming a condition. Community framing ("many Catholic women") is used wherever a shared experience is described.
- **Banned vocabulary:** no bad luck, curse, evil, hex, Jezebel, spiritual attack, spiritual warfare or generational curse. Approved vocabulary used throughout: blessed, blessing, grace, protection, faith, sacred, devotion, Mary, Mother, intercession, family, daughter, granddaughter, grandmother.
- **Proof:** zero testimonials, zero star ratings, zero review counts, zero family counts, zero batch percentages, zero reservations-remaining, zero countdowns. The one soft popularity claim is quarantined as an optional variant on 6E and flagged as his call.
- **Claims:** only the four approved ones appear, hand-blessed in the Benedictine tradition before it ships, free US shipping, 30-day full refund including shipping, ships within 24 hours. No outcome is promised from wearing or praying. No medical, financial or miracle claim.
- **Punctuation:** no em-dashes and no en-dashes in this document. Hyphens appear only in compound words such as hand-blessed, hand-knotted, double-strand, gold-plated, tarnish-resistant, three-medal, two-pack, silver-tone, gold-tone, 30-day, 18K gold plated.
- **Mobile:** every question is one line, every option is eight words or fewer, each question screen holds one question and three or four options with nothing else competing for the fold.
