## RUN ORDER AND QA

Generate in this order and stop at the first failure rather than burning credits down the list:

1. `pair-product` first. It is the simplest scene, so if the geometry lock is going to hold anywhere it holds here. If the chain comes back thick or flat, the reference weighting is too low and every later shot will fail the same way.
2. `pair-hero1`, `pair-macro`. Object-led shots that confirm the front junction and the cross to medal ratio.
3. `pair-worn`, `pair-hand`. The hardest, because the model wants to simplify the chain at distance and turn the junction into a single ring.
4. `pair-gift`, `pair-blessing`. Scene-led, most forgiving.

**Reject and regenerate on any of these:**

- The chain reads thick, woven, fabric-like, or flat and ribbon-like at any distance
- Any large O-ring or decorative ring in the front cluster
- More than two end caps, or more than one clasp
- The cross is inflated, tubular or high-gloss instead of slim and flat-faced
- Any figure of Christ on the cross
- The medal looks as tall as the cross, or larger than half its height
- The junction has migrated to the back of the neck
- Any legible text, card, packaging or supplier branding in frame
- The necklace is coiled small enough to read as a bracelet

**Compliance check:** these prompts avoid personal-attribute framing, contain no health or healing depiction, no financial imagery, no before and after staging, and no fear or occult styling; imagery is limited to devotional objects, candles, hands in prayer and a woman wearing a necklace, which is within Meta's accepted range for the faith vertical.
