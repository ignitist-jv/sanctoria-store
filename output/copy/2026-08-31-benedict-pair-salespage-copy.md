DIVIDER LABEL: Why Two

H2: One says whose you are. *One asks Him to guard them.*

INTRO P1: For more than fifteen centuries, families have turned to Saint Benedict when they wanted their people covered. His medal has been set into the foundations of houses, hung over cribs, tucked above a car visor, carried in a soldier's pocket. It is what the Church has always reached for when a mother says protect them.

INTRO P2: But a medal on its own is a request without a name on it. So this one hangs beside a plain Latin cross, both of them on the same ring, both of them under the same palm.

ITEM 1 LABEL: The Cross · Whose You Are
ITEM 1 BODY: Smooth polished arms, no figure on it, no engraving. It is the cross as a statement, not as a scene. It is the part that says you belong to Him before you ask Him for anything, and it is the part a stranger recognizes without a word from you.

ITEM 2 LABEL: The Medal · The Asking
ITEM 2 BODY: A round Saint Benedict medal in raised relief, the traditional letters ringed around the rim. Those letters are the initials of the old Latin prayer of protection the Church has prayed for centuries. This is the part that carries the names. The people you say at two in the morning.

CLOSING P: Your palm covers both at once. Belief and petition in a single gesture you were already making.

OFFERING NOTE LABEL: ★ What The Letters Mean

They are the initials of the **traditional Latin prayer of Saint Benedict**, a prayer of protection the Church has used for centuries. ## SECTION 15: FAQ

Q1: Is it really blessed?
A1: Yes. Every Benedict Pair is hand-blessed in the Benedictine tradition before it leaves us, one small batch at a time. It arrives already blessed, ready to put on or to give exactly as it comes out of the box.

Q2: Is this a charm? Does it do something on its own?
A2: No, and we will not tell you otherwise. A sacramental is not a charm and it has no power of its own. It is a blessed sign that turns the heart toward God and reminds the one wearing it to keep asking. The grace belongs to God. What this does is make sure the reminder is at the center of your chest all day instead of in a drawer.

Q3: Will it tarnish or turn my skin green?
A3: No. It is 18K gold plated over stainless steel, not a cheap alloy and not a plating that flakes. It is waterproof and tarnish resistant, which is why we tell you to shower in it and sleep in it. The one you put on tomorrow should still look like this next Christmas.

Q4: How long is the chain, and will a man wear it?
A4: The double-strand chain is made to sit at the center of the chest, high enough to disappear under a collar or a blouse, and it fastens with a lobster clasp. Men wear this one. It is flat, warm gold, and quiet under a shirt, and there is nothing on it that reads as women's jewelry. Husbands and sons who would never wear a chain wear this one.

Q5: How fast does it ship?
A5: We ship within 24 hours of your order once your batch has been blessed. Most families in the US receive theirs in 5 to 7 days, with free shipping.

Q6: What if it is not right for me?
A6: Then you owe us nothing. You have a full 30 days for a complete refund, your shipping included. Write to support@sanctoriastore.com and we will take care of it. Wear it every one of those 30 days first. That is the only way to know.

Q7: Can I give it as a gift?
A7: That is what most people do with the second one. It arrives already blessed, in a box you can hand over as it is, with the prayer card inside so they can read what they are wearing. There is no size to ask about, so nothing gives the surprise away.
