# Sanctoria · Email Header Image Prompts

**Date:** 2026-10-06
**For:** the header image at the top of every Sanctoria email sent from Kit
**Spec:** 1200 x 400px (600 x 200 at 2x retina), 3:1
**Audience:** buyers of all four devotions plus chat funnel leads who have bought nothing. This is a BRAND header, not a product header.
**Logo:** `site/images/logo.png`, 478 x 220 transparent PNG, gold bevelled cross above a black and gold serif "Sanctoria" with a gold flourish rule. Composited on AFTER generation. No text is ever generated.

---

## WHY NO PRODUCT APPEARS IN ANY OF THESE

Deliberate, and it is a real trade. A brand header with product is more evocative than one without. The alternative is a generated bracelet, chain or rosary that contradicts four real SKUs, and this project has already paid for that lesson once: all eleven necklace images were regenerated on 2026-09-09 because the originals showed a woven mesh rope and a large O-ring the product does not have. A header showing a recognisable but wrong devotion is worse than one showing none.

Two specific traps, both banned in the prompts: a crucifix with a corpus would contradict the necklace, which is a plain Latin cross with no Christ figure; and a full rosary would contradict the real cord rosary.

## 1 · RECOMMENDATION · "Morning Linen"

Overhead flat lay, cream linen, symmetric, nothing that can be misread as a product.

```
Overhead flat-lay photograph, camera directly perpendicular to the surface, no perspective
tilt. The entire surface is a single sheet of soft washed cream linen in warm off-white
#F7F3EC, filling the frame edge to edge on all four sides with no table edge, no floor, no
wall and no border of any kind visible. The linen has a gentle visible weave and two or three
very soft wide folds running diagonally from upper left to lower right, the folds raised only
enough to catch light, flat and relaxed rather than crumpled.

Morning daylight enters from the upper left at a low angle, soft and diffused as if through a
sheer curtain in a lived-in house. Shadows are long, soft-edged and extremely pale, never
darker than a warm greige around #E3DACB. There is no hard light, no spotlight, no beam, no
visible light ray, no vignette, no glow.

COMPOSITION, strictly as follows. The frame divides into three horizontal bands.
- The TOP band, occupying the upper 15 percent of the height, is clean unbroken linen with
  nothing on it at all.
- The MIDDLE band, occupying from 15 percent to 72 percent of the height and the full width,
  is clean unbroken linen with nothing on it at all. This band is deliberately empty.
- The BOTTOM band, occupying the lower 28 percent of the height, carries a single quiet row of
  simple domestic objects sitting on the linen, arranged loosely symmetrically about the
  vertical centre line and all of them cropped by the bottom edge of the frame so that only
  their upper portions are visible.

The objects in that bottom band, from left to right:
- At roughly one sixth of the frame width from the left, a small shallow dish of plain
  unglazed stoneware in a pale warm bisque #EFE6D6, about half of it cut off by the bottom
  edge. Its visible width is about one ninth of the total frame width.
- At roughly one third from the left, a sprig of fresh olive leaves lying flat on the linen,
  four or five slim grey-green leaves #A9AE92, the sprig about one seventh of the frame width
  long, soft and slightly out of focus.
- Centred at the bottom edge, a loosely folded corner of a second linen cloth in a slightly
  warmer sand tone #EFE4D2, its fold line reading as one soft horizontal.
- At roughly two thirds from the left, a second shorter olive sprig mirroring the first.
- At roughly five sixths from the left, a small plain cream ceramic cup seen from above,
  empty, no pattern, no handle visible, no liquid, its rim diameter about one eighth of the
  frame width, again cut by the bottom edge.
All of these objects are rendered softly, slightly below the plane of critical focus, so that
they read as colour and shape rather than as detail. Nothing in the bottom band is shiny,
reflective, metallic or jewelry-like.

CLEAR ZONE FOR THE LOGO. The central rectangle of the frame spanning horizontally from 27
percent to 73 percent of the width, and vertically from 12 percent to 68 percent of the
height, must be completely empty linen. No object, no fold crest, no shadow edge, no texture
change, no tonal shift crosses into that rectangle. Across that whole rectangle the linen
holds an even light value, roughly #F7F3EC to #F2EDE3, with no area darker than #EDE6D9. That
rectangle is about 25 percent of the total image area and it is reserved for a logo that will
be composited on afterwards.

PALETTE. Dominated by cream #F7F3EC and a slightly warmer cream #F1EADD. Shadow tones no
darker than #E3DACB. One muted bronze accent is permitted only in the bottom band, the colour
of the dish rim catching light, around #9A7B4F, and it must occupy less than two percent of
the frame. No gold, no yellow, no saturated colour anywhere. The overall impression is cream
on cream, quiet, warm, almost monochrome.

CAMERA AND RENDER. Photographic, medium format look, 50mm equivalent lens, f/4, focus plane on
the linen in the middle band so the bottom band falls gently soft. Fine natural film grain,
no digital sharpening halos. Natural colour, no colour grading, no teal or orange push, no
HDR, no high contrast. Exposure sits bright and open, highlights never blowing to pure white
#FFFFFF, shadows never going grey or blue.

CRITICAL: there is absolutely no text, lettering, writing, wordmark, logo, monogram, signature,
watermark, number, label, tag or any typographic element anywhere in this image. The frame
contains no readable characters of any kind.

Wide panoramic banner format, aspect ratio 3:1, final delivery 1200 x 400 pixels.
```

**Logo clear zone:** centred, 27 to 73 percent horizontally, 12 to 68 percent vertically, about a quarter of the frame. The lockup at roughly 460px wide in a 1200px file sits inside it with air on all four sides, and the cream under it keeps the black letterforms crisp.

**On crop:** symmetric about the vertical centre, so a client that trims the sides or letterboxes the top loses only empty linen. The only content is in the bottom band, which is already cropped by design, so a further bottom crop reads as a deliberate tighter frame.

**At 600px on a phone:** a warm cream field with a soft row of shapes along the base. Nothing depends on detail so nothing mushes. The risk is the opposite one, that it reads as almost nothing, which is why the bottom band and the fold shadows exist at all.

## 2 · ALTERNATE A · "The Weave"

Pure material, zero objects. For when the flat lay reads as too styled, or for a send where the logo must carry everything alone.

```
Extreme close macro photograph of a single piece of soft washed cream linen, filling the
entire frame edge to edge with no object, no prop, no edge of cloth, no surface beneath and no
background visible. The cloth is the whole image.

The weave is clearly legible as a regular warm cream crosshatch, individual threads soft and
slightly irregular, a few loose fibres standing proud and catching light. One slow soft fold
runs from the lower left corner up toward the right, rising and falling gently so that one
side of it lifts into light and the other falls into the palest shade. A second much fainter
fold sits near the right edge. The folds are relaxed and heavy, the drape of a laundered
tablecloth, not creased paper.

Light is early morning daylight raking in from the left at a shallow angle, soft and
directional, so the weave texture is revealed by grazing light rather than by contrast. The
lit cream reads #F7F3EC lifting to #FBF8F2 at the crest of the fold. The shaded slope reads
#EDE4D5 at its deepest, and nothing in the frame is darker than #E3DACB. The gradient across
the frame is gradual and even, left slightly brighter than right, with no hotspot and no
vignette.

CLEAR ZONE FOR THE LOGO. The central rectangle spanning horizontally from 25 percent to 75
percent of the width and vertically from 15 percent to 70 percent of the height must be the
calmest part of the image. No fold crest, no fold shadow, no loose fibre and no tonal step
crosses into it. Across that rectangle the linen is near-flat in value, between #F7F3EC and
#F3EEE4, so that dark lettering composited on top of it will read cleanly. The folds live
entirely in the outer left sixth and the outer right quarter of the frame.

CAMERA AND RENDER. Photographic macro, 100mm equivalent lens, f/5.6, focus on the weave in the
central area with a very gentle falloff toward both short edges. Natural fine grain. Flat
natural colour with no grading, no contrast boost, no clarity or structure slider, no HDR. The
tone is warm and the white balance is slightly warm, never cool, never grey, never blue.

CRITICAL: no text, no lettering, no stitching that forms letters, no monogram, no embroidered
initials, no label, no tag, no watermark, no numbers, no typographic marks of any kind
anywhere in the frame.

Wide panoramic banner format, aspect ratio 3:1, final delivery 1200 x 400 pixels.
```

**On crop:** the safest of the three by a wide margin. Every crop returns another piece of the same cloth, so it cannot break.

**At 600px on a phone:** the weave softens into a warm cream texture, which is the intended outcome. It never looks like a failed load, because the cream is clearly a material rather than a blank.

## 3 · ALTERNATE B · "The Sill"

An actual room with depth. For sends that need warmth and the sense of a real house rather than a styled surface.

```
Photograph of a quiet domestic interior, shot level with the surface rather than from above,
camera at the height of a side table in an ordinary home.

Along the lower third of the frame runs the top of a pale wooden side table, a warm bleached
oak in a soft tan #D9C7A8, its grain soft and its surface slightly worn. The table top is
mostly empty. At the far LEFT of the frame, cropped by the left edge so only part of it is
seen, stands a small plain terracotta pot in a muted dusty clay #C9A287 holding a single
unremarkable green houseplant with three or four broad soft leaves, out of focus. At the far
RIGHT of the frame, cropped by the right edge, stands a plain cream ceramic jug, no pattern,
no handle detail, also out of focus. Both objects are strongly defocused, reading as colour
masses only.

Behind the table, filling the upper two thirds of the frame, is a plain cream wall #F1EADD. In
the right third of the frame a window is visible, heavily out of focus, its glass a soft blown
white with no view resolved beyond it and no window bars forming a cross shape. A sheer
off-white curtain hangs beside it, lifted very slightly, the folds soft and vertical. Morning
sun falls through and lays a single soft diagonal patch of warm light across the cream wall
and onto the table, its edges completely feathered, no hard-edged shape, no visible sunbeam
through air, no dust motes, no shaft.

CLEAR ZONE FOR THE LOGO. The rectangle spanning horizontally from 18 percent to 64 percent of
the width and vertically from 10 percent to 60 percent of the height is plain unbroken cream
wall. Nothing crosses it: no object, no curtain fold, no window frame, no picture, no shelf,
no sharp shadow edge. Across that whole rectangle the wall stays within #F7F3EC to #F0E9DC and
never drops below #EAE1D1. That rectangle is roughly 23 percent of the frame area and is
reserved for a logo composited afterwards. The window, the curtain and the light patch all sit
to the RIGHT of 64 percent of the width.

PALETTE. Cream #F7F3EC and #F1EADD across the wall, warm pale oak #D9C7A8 on the table, one
soft bronze note #9A7B4F where the table edge catches light, a single muted sage green in the
plant. No gold, no saturated colour, no warm orange cast, no cool blue cast. The room reads
calm and softly lit, never moody.

CAMERA AND RENDER. Photographic, 35mm equivalent lens, f/2.0, focus plane on the table top in
the left-centre, so the window, the curtain and both objects fall well out of focus with a
smooth round bokeh. Natural fine grain, natural colour, no grading, no HDR, no vignette, no
heavy contrast. The brightest point in the frame is the window and it is a soft warm white,
not clipped to pure #FFFFFF.

CRITICAL: no people, no faces, no hands, no reflections of a person in the window or any
surface. No text, lettering, book spines with titles, labels, signage, wall art with writing,
calendar, watermark or numbers anywhere in the frame.

Wide panoramic banner format, aspect ratio 3:1, final delivery 1200 x 400 pixels.
```

**On crop:** the weakest of the three, because it is asymmetric. A right-side crop takes the window and kills the light story. The cream wall runs to the top and left, so vertical and left crops are safe. Use only if the clients on this list are known to behave.

**At 600px on a phone:** holds up well. The whole thing is built from large soft masses and one diagonal of light, so nothing small matters.

## 5 · GENERATOR NOTES

Written for **Higgsfield `nano_banana_pro`**, already used on this project, which responds well to long declarative prompts with explicit percentage placement and hex values.

- **It does not give a clean 3:1.** Render at 21:9 and crop 3:1 out of the centre. The percentages above are written for the FINAL 3:1 frame, so crop first and then check the clear zone. If the crop eats the bottom band in the Recommendation, re-render with the bottom band moved up to 70 percent rather than fixing it in the crop.
- **It renders text unprompted,** especially on cloth, ceramics and anything resembling packaging. The "CRITICAL: no text" line inside the positive prompt is doing more work than the negative prompt. Keep it in both.

Elsewhere:
- **Midjourney:** strip the hex values, it ignores them; use named colours, add `--ar 3:1 --style raw --s 50`, move the negative into `--no`. Expect more seeds, the clear zone is not reliably respected.
- **Flux or Ideogram:** keep the hexes, they help. Ideogram tries hardest to add text, so run it with text rendering explicitly off.
- **DALL-E 3:** rewrites prompts and adds props. Halve the prompt and lead with the clear zone instruction.
- **Any model:** anchor sizes to in-frame ratios as written ("one ninth of the total frame width"), never to centimetres or millimetres. Logged in LEARNINGS.md on 2026-09-09.

## 6 · RISKS, NAMED

1. **The olive sprigs are the weak point of the Recommendation.** Olive reads Mediterranean and Benedictine, which is why it is there, but rendered crisply it can push the frame toward a wellness or skincare brand. If the first renders feel spa-like, drop the olive and keep the dish, the folded cloth and the cup. The image survives it.
2. **The window in Alternate B can produce a cross.** Window muntins form crosses, and a background cross on a Catholic brand header reads as deliberate and would be a different cross from the one on the actual product. Banned explicitly in the prompt. Check every render before approving.
3. **Cream on cream can go grey.** All three live or die on white balance. Sample the clear zone with a picker before approving. More than about 2 percent toward blue and it looks dirty beside the real cream on the site, and the logo will look pasted on.
4. **No jewelry at all is a deliberate cost.** See the section at the top.
5. **Hands were considered and rejected.** Mature hands on linen would warm the frame considerably, but hands are where generators fail most visibly, and a sixth finger on the header of every email to the whole list is an unrecoverable kind of wrong. If the warmth is wanted, run it as a separate fourth test against a much higher pass bar.
6. **Dark mode is unresolved and is a template question, not an image question.** Several clients invert or darken the background behind this image, and a cream header on an inverted dark email looks like a bright slab. None of these three images can solve it. Decide whether the logo file needs a light-on-dark variant.
