## VOICE RULES THAT GOVERN EVERY LINE BELOW

These are build constraints, not copy. Read them before wiring.

1. **No line claims a live human is typing.** Nothing says "I am reading your answers", "let me look", "one second while I check", "typing...". Every line is true when a script says it.
2. **No line claims clergy.** No Father, no Brother, no monk, no chaplain, no "I am a priest", no blessing conferred in the chat, no absolution, no spiritual direction. Blessing is only ever described as something done to the object before it ships, which is the brand's standing claim.
3. **A name can be attached later without any line becoming false.** Every line is written so it works prefixed by a named guide, for example "Teresa from Sanctoria", and still works with no name at all. Nowhere does a line depend on who is speaking.
4. **She tells us, we never tell her.** No bubble asserts her religion, her health, her money or her family circumstances. Every reflection is a reflection of what she just tapped.
5. **Typing indicator is allowed, delay is allowed, "is typing" text is not.** Use three animated dots with no words. A dot animation is decoration. The words "is typing" are a claim.
6. Bubble arrival: about 500ms to 900ms between bubbles, chips appear only after the last bubble in a group has landed. Never queue two questions at once.

### shortWhy, LIVE
Changed, not kept. "Show me the other three" is the one place she compares products side by side, and it was the only place the axis she had just decided on would have been invisible. Form leads, the moment sits behind it.

Worn, worn, worn, held. The fourth breaks the pattern on purpose, because the fourth is not worn.

### WHAT THIS MAKES WORSE, NAMED
- **The question is colder, and "The moment news lands" is gone as a chip.** That was probably the most-tapped string in the funnel. The weight moved into the ack, into the shortWhy, and into the ask's "or hold", which is the one piece of emotional signalling a form question can honestly do: it tells her one of the four is not jewelry at all. The acks now carry this question's warmth almost alone. **If conversion drops at question 2 rather than at the result, this is the cause.**
- **The twin "On my wrist" openings cost scan speed.** She must read to the comma to tell the first two apart. That is the price of making the split visible. If tap data shows hesitation at question 2, differentiate the openings instead: "On my wrist, when I pray" and "Never off my wrist, ever".
- **Form is a weaker commercial sorter than desire, and the rosary pays for it.** It now routes on a preference rather than on the heirloom motive that actually sells it, so `rosary.lead` carries selling work the question used to pre-sell.
- **One conflation that cannot be removed in copy.** "I wear no jewelry" and "I want something to hand down" arrive through the same chip, because the rosary is the only non-worn product. Those are different women and they get the same page. Catalogue limit, not a copy limit.
- **One string to watch.** "never taken off" is her statement of intent, not a durability or water-resistance claim, but it sits one reading away from one. Softer version if it ever needs it: "On my wrist, all day long".
