# Sanctoria — St Benedict Heritage / Meaning Batch (5 cold ads)

**Date:** 2026-07-30
**Project:** sanctoria-store
**Test goal:** Test a genuinely new HERITAGE / MEANING angle on cold traffic, story and mechanism led, distinct from the recently killed "EveryDetailHasMeaning" infographic. Point at the split-test URL `https://sanctoriastore.com/go/`.
**Traffic:** Cold, T1, women 35-65+.
**Rules:** Claim-free (no efficacy, no fear vocabulary), no pricing, no em/en dashes, real St Benedict Marian Bracelet in every image (ebony beads + antique bronze St Benedict medal + bronze crucifix + bronze Miraculous Medal + extender chain/lobster clasp).

## Phase 1 — Performance summary (from 2026-07-30 scale-or-kill)
- Winners: `AQuietDevotion` 2.98x (quiet ritual), `Chiaroscuro` 2.74x (the "Weight of Devotion" heritage/craft piece). The reverent, craft-forward heritage tone converts.
- On cooldown (recently paused): EveryDetailHasMeaning, CarryYourFaith, Minimalist, BoldEditorial, PassItDown, HandcraftedWithDevotion, AGiftOfFaith, PrayerYouWear, WhyIWearItDaily. SacredHeritage took 1 sale (2.76x) before pausing.

## Phase 2 — Strategic diagnosis
- Awareness = solution-aware (they know St Benedict medals, many own a cheap one). Sophistication = 4-5 (hammered with protection/exorcism). Lead type = mechanism / story, never a bare protection claim.
- Problem mechanism: most Catholics own or want a St Benedict medal but never learned what it says or where it came from; the meaning got cheapened by mass-market versions.
- Solution mechanism: this bracelet carries the real medal with its full inscription and 1,500-year story, so wearing it is carrying that heritage knowingly.
- Dominant emotion: reverence and belonging, a quiet pride of finally knowing.
- Competitor gap: every competitor weaponizes the medal (exorcism, demons, curses). None tell the reverent origin story or simply decode the medal as heritage. That clean educational lane is open.
- Build on proven language: "weight in your hand," "the relief is sharp, the inscription readable," "real ebony and bronze," "1,500 years," "blessed in Rome."
