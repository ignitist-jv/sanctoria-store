# Gifting UGC Statics — 5 NEW ads (2026-09-11)

Five brand-new UGC-style statics. New stories, new speakers, no reuse.

## Why these five

The best performing ad in account history is a UGC static where a mother gave one to her adult
daughter: **2 sales in a day, 13.8x, $76.50 average order value**, roughly double the $37
single. **Every converting order in this account has been a multi-pack.** Second best is a
men's-angle static at 4.67x.

So these five push hard into the two proven seams: **gifting / multi-pack** and **men**.
Three of the five put more than one bracelet in the frame.

By contrast, the polished brand statics (illustrated scenes, triptychs, macros, product heroes,
infographics) have spent roughly $300 across ~20 ad sets for **zero sales**. That is why these
are deliberately amateur phone snapshots with no brand overlay.

## Build spec

- Format: **UGC**, SQUARE 1:1, 1080x1080
- Destination **/bracelet/** · CTA **Shop Now** · $5/day · campaign PAUSED
- Targeting held constant at T1_MF_35-65+ so the creative is the only variable
- Built from the real store product photo
- **No brand text overlay**, by design. The overlay is what makes an ad look like an ad.

## Compliance notes

**Written defensively because a previous ad in this exact format was REJECTED.** That ad was
about a husband and the rejection reason is unknown (the ad was deleted, so there is no record
in the account to read). Without the reason, these five were written more conservatively than
the approved batch:

- **No ages stated for any third party**, and first-person ages minimised. The approved UGC
  statics used first-person ages ("I'm 54"), but since the rejected one described a husband's
  age and church attendance, both were avoided here.
- **No description of anyone else's religious practice or church attendance.** `04_MyDadWearsIt`
  deliberately keeps the father described by what he does with the object, never by how devout
  he is.
- No em or en dashes.
- No efficacy or supernatural claims. Nothing protects, heals, wards anything off or brings luck.
- No fear, curse or spiritual-warfare vocabulary.
- No fabricated scarcity. The shipping caveats in 01 and 03 are honest speaker notes, not urgency.
- No fabricated testimonials, names or ratings. First-person narration only, the same
  construction as the approved and converting ads in this account.
- No second-person assertions about the reader's faith or practice.

**If the rejection reason surfaces**, tell me and I will re-audit all five against it. It is the
single most useful compliance datapoint available and it is currently unknown.
