# VOC Angle Research — adult Catholic pain points — 2026-08-31

Method: 3 parallel research agents across Catholic Answers forums, Catholic press reader-letters (CatholicPhilly, GrowChristians, Aleteia, CatholicMom), devotional essays, and St Benedict bracelet product reviews (Catholic Company, Sisters of Carmel, Amazon). Reddit blocked automated fetch; quotes are real first-person VOC from accessible sources. Filtered through the Ad Angle Policy (peace/devotion/blessing lane; NO fear/curse/evil/warfare/efficacy).

## Three master pain themes
1. FAMILY: adult children / grandchildren drifting from the Church. #1 prayer request Catholic clergy receive. Grief + guilt + helplessness + hope (St Monica).
2. PERSONAL: spiritual dryness, "I feel nothing when I pray," forgetting to pray, feeling far from God, shame. Longing + discouragement.
3. LIFE/SACRAMENTAL: comfort in hard seasons (grief/illness), why people wear the medal (protection desire -> must reframe to PAX/peace/watched-over), gift-to-bless, identity, homecoming.

## Compliance master rule (from all 3 streams)
The St Benedict medal's folk identity is "protection from evil / spiritual warfare." That entire vocabulary is Meta-banned (killed the old domain). Satisfy the SAME emotion (security, peace, being watched over) by pointing at God's presence + the wearer's faith + peace, never at a threat repelled or a power in the object. Lean on PAX (Peace, the actual Benedictine motto on the medal).
Banned: protection, protect, evil, demon, satan, curse, warfare, attack, armor, defense, ward off, "keeps you safe from," any efficacy ("works," "brings them back," "heals").
Safe: peace, PAX, blessed, grace, devotion, hope, comfort, watched over, held, close to God, prayed for, with you, never alone, faith you can wear, heritage.

### TIER 1 (build first)
1. TheDrySeason / "Wear it anyway" — spiritual dryness, "feel nothing when I pray." Untapped, huge, deeply relatable, fully compliant (steadfast devotion). Names the STRUGGLE, distinct from the generic-devotion winner. VOC: "It always just feels so empty... going through the motions, and there is nothing there." Hook: "Some mornings you pray and feel nothing. Keep going anyway." Mechanism: a devotion you can hold even when the feelings aren't there.
2. NotAlone / StMonica — the lonely praying mother + never-give-up hope. Combines isolation + St Monica perseverance. Massive audience, intense, compliant, net-new vs our "pass it down" generational angle. VOC: "I very much needed to know that I am not alone"; "the child of so many tears shall never perish." Hook: "You are not the only mother praying for a child who stopped going to Mass. St Monica prayed for seventeen years. She never gave up."
3. WhenWordsFail / comfort in the hard season — grief, illness, a frightening diagnosis. Net-new (grief entry), universal, compliant emotional comfort. VOC: "The Rosary has really been a blessing particularly when I don't have words"; "It was like my mama Mary came over to hold me for a while." Hook: "There are days the only prayer you can manage is to hold on. This gives you something to hold."

### TIER 2 (bench)
4. PAX / the peace on the medal — anxiety/overwhelm, the real Benedictine motto. Authentic + educational + compliant. Hook: "There is one word pressed into this medal. PAX. Peace."
5. TheReminder / "you keep meaning to pray" — consistency/habit anchor. Safest, relatable; adjacent to the devotion winner. Hook: "You mean to pray more. Then the day gets away from you."
6. ComeHome / the revert — returning to faith, "welcomed back." Net-new audience (reverts/lapsed returning). Hook: "Coming back to your faith after a long time away? Mark the day."
7. YouDidntFail — guilt reassurance for parents of lapsed kids. Powerful but narrower; can fold into NotAlone. Hook: "You did not fail them. Keep loving them. Keep praying."
