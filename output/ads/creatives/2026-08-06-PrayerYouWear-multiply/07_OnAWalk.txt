=== AD 07: It Goes Where You Go (on a walk) ===

PRIMARY TEXT:
A rosary stays on the nightstand. This comes with you out the door.

On the morning walk. To the mailbox. Down the garden path. The St Benedict Marian Bracelet is on your wrist for all of it, a prayer you can reach for without breaking stride.

Your faith, out in the fresh air, right there where you can hold it.

Come look.
