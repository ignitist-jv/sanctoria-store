## The mechanism being multiplied

**WornNotStored.** Nearly every Catholic household has a blessed Saint Benedict medal
forgotten in a drawer, a jewellery box or a glovebox. It was given at a baptism, a
confirmation, or a funeral. It never gets worn because it is small, fiddly, or feels like it
belongs to another generation. The Triptych is the one you actually wear.
The contrast is **STORED versus WORN**. Devotion used, not kept.

Tone kept from the winner: masculine, plain-spoken, unsentimental, working-class dignity.
Ungendered but the imagery skews male, which opens a Catholic-men audience.
