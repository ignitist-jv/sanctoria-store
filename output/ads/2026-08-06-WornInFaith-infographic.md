# WornInFaith — Benefit Infographic (compliant reframe of the Gleetoria claims ad)

**Date:** 2026-08-06
**Type:** New format test. Borrows the STRUCTURE of a competitor (Gleetoria) benefit-radial infographic: bracelet as hero in the center, benefit callouts with arrows radiating out, strong headline, trust line at the bottom. Everything else rebuilt to be fully compliant.

**Removed from the competitor original (do NOT use):**
- "Spiritual attacks gone in 9 days" (efficacy + time claim)
- "Or your money back" tied to a cure claim
- "Breaks generational curses", "Repels dark energy", "Restores lost faith" (fear vocabulary + efficacy claims, banned on the Sanctoria domain)
- "Protects your family" as a supernatural-efficacy claim
- The "as featured in EWTN / Catholic News / The Tablet / National Catholic Register / Aleteia" press bar (fabricated endorsements)
- "Not a medical product" disclaimer (no claim is made, so it is not needed)

**Kept (the winning format):** warm premium look, bracelet hero, 4 benefit callouts with arrows, headline, real trust line.

**Compliant benefit callouts (devotional meaning + product truth, no efficacy claims):**
1. GUARD YOUR FAMILY IN PRAYER
2. KEEP YOUR FAITH CLOSE
3. THE BLESSED ST BENEDICT MEDAL
4. A DEVOTION TO PASS DOWN

**Image headline:** WORN IN FAITH. CARRIED IN PRAYER.
**Image subhead:** The St Benedict Marian Bracelet
**Trust line (real facts only):** Free US shipping · 30-day money-back guarantee
**Brand mark:** SANCTORIA · Blessed Devotionals

**Standing rules kept:** claim-free, no pricing on image or in text, real bracelet in the image, no em/en dashes, destination /bracelet-v2/.
