# New Territories — 5 New-Angle Test Ads (2026-09-07)

Five deliberately different angles. Every angle this brand has run to date sits in six
emotional territories aimed at the same devout Catholic woman in the same moment
(legacy, protection, calm devotion, heritage, intercession, generic gifting). Those are
now producing high CTR and poor conversion. **These five change WHO buys or WHY they buy
now, rather than redressing the same feeling.**

- Format: PHOTO (lifestyle), SQUARE 1:1, 1080x1080
- Destination: /bracelet/
- CTA button: Shop Now
- Targeting held constant at T1_MF_35-65+ across all five, so the ANGLE is the only variable
- Images built from the real store product photo, so the ad matches the page
- No text baked into the AI images; headline and CTA overlaid via HTML/CSS + headless Chrome

## Compliance notes

- No em or en dashes anywhere.
- No efficacy claims. The bracelet is never said to protect, heal, ward off anything or
  make prayers work. ThePoisonedCup frames the legend strictly as tradition ("as the story
  is handed down", "whether you take it as history or as legend").
- No fear, curse, evil or spiritual-warfare vocabulary.
- No fabricated scarcity, urgency or stock counts. TheConfirmationGift gets its timeliness
  from a real occasion, not an invented deadline.
- No fabricated testimonials, reviews or ratings.
- No second-person assertions about the reader's faith or religious status. **TheWayBack was
  rewritten during QC** because an earlier draft said "you do not need to have been to
  confession recently", which implies the reader is lapsed and is a Meta personal-attributes
  violation. It is now entirely first person.
- **ThePoisonedCup was corrected during QC** for a factual error: an earlier draft said the
  Church had kept the medal for fifteen hundred years. Benedict LIVED about 1500 years ago,
  but the medal itself dates to roughly the eleventh century, with the familiar Jubilee
  design formalised in 1880. It now says the faithful have worn his medal "for centuries".
