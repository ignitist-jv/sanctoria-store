# SungByName video batch 2: four new recipients and occasions
Date: 2026-10-12. Format: VID, 25 to 30 seconds each. Status: COPY APPROVED, films not yet produced.

## Where this came from

Pulled 78 live PrayerSong ads from the Meta Ad Library on 2026-10-12.

| What | Finding |
|---|---|
| Longevity | Running continuously since Nov 2025, about 11 months. Deeply profitable. |
| Median video length | **3:01** (range 39s to 4:40) |
| Distinct hooks | 30, differing almost entirely by RECIPIENT and OCCASION, not mechanism |
| Description | "50% off limited time only" on nearly every ad |
| Market | "Christian", not Catholic |

**Their formula has three parts. We can legally copy one.**

1. Past-tense customer reaction ("she couldn't believe", "got them bawling",
   "tears in this man's eyes"). FORBIDDEN here. We have sold zero units.
2. "50% off limited time only". FORBIDDEN. No discounts, no deadlines.
3. **Recipient and occasion spread.** This we can take, and it is where their
   30 variants actually differ.

A fourth lesson was offered and declined: their videos are ten times longer
than ours and actually play the song. TS chose to keep this batch at 25 to 30
seconds. Recorded here because it remains the clearest structural difference
between us and an advertiser profitable for eleven months.

**Ponds already covered, deliberately not repeated:** son, daughter, husband
and marriage, name card (live `SungByName`); deceased husband, mother and
brother (`SungForTheHolySouls`).

**Two options were dropped on inspection.** "All the siblings together" is not
buildable: the product carries one or two first names, so a four-name song is a
promise the order form cannot keep. "A wife" was left alone because the buyer
there is a husband, and these point at Catholic women 45+.

Headline `Create Your Custom Prayer Song Now`, description
`$39, one time. Sent within 24 hours.`, CTA Shop Now, destination
`https://sanctoriastore.com/prayer-song/` carrying the url_tags string.

**Note:** the copywriter proposed `T1_F` (women only). Every live ad set is
`T1_MF`. F-only is defensible for a godmother and a daughter buying for parents,
but it is a targeting change, not just a naming one. Decide before building.

**On the caption names:** Michael, Nora, John and Eileen, and Clare are
sample-song names, the same convention as Daniel in the existing films. Nothing
claims any of them is a customer, so no burned-in disclosure is needed. That
changes the moment a film shows a real person's reaction.
