# FaithForGenerations — Gold Hero Pedestal (compliant reframe of the "Generational Curses" ad)

**Date:** 2026-08-06
**Type:** New format test. Borrows the STRUCTURE of a Gleetoria ad: gold metallic hero headline, bracelet on a lit reflective pedestal against a moody dramatic background, three icon-badge callouts at the bottom. Rebuilt fully compliant.

**Removed from the competitor original (do NOT use):**
- "Your shield against GENERATIONAL CURSES" (fear vocabulary + efficacy)
- "Breaks generational bondage" (fear + efficacy)
- "Blessed for spiritual protection" as a supernatural-efficacy claim
- "Covers your entire family" as an efficacy claim
- "Not a medical product" disclaimer + the fake press logo

**Compliant angle:** A Faith For Every Generation. Carries the family/legacy/protection EMOTION (which maps to the proven generational mechanism) without the banned words. Framing = keep faith close, guard your family in prayer, pass the devotion down.

**Kept (the winning format):** gold metallic hero headline (eyebrow + big line), bracelet on a lit pedestal, moody dramatic background, 3 icon badges, brand mark.

**Standing rules kept:** claim-free, no pricing, real bracelet in every image, no em/en dashes, no fake press. Destination /bracelet-v2/. 1 ad per ad set.
