## Format decision

All nine are **UGC statics**, square 1:1, **no brand text overlay**.

This is evidence-led, not stylistic. In this account a UGC static produced 2 sales in a day at
13.8x, while polished brand statics (illustrated scenes, triptych heroes, macros, infographics)
have spent roughly $300 across ~20 ad sets for zero sales. Each funnel also gets one
**gifting / multi-pack** angle, because every converting order in this account has been a
multi-pack.

## Compliance notes

- No em or en dashes anywhere.
- **NO PROTECTION LANGUAGE in any of the nine**, despite the Triptych page living at /guard/
  and the necklace being literally named "Double Protection Necklace". The pages use stronger
  wording than the ads are permitted to. Not one ad says protect, guard, shield, safe or
  watch over.
- No efficacy or supernatural claims. `CarryingAName` states the limit outright: "It doesn't
  do anything. I want to be honest about that."
- No fear, curse or spiritual-warfare vocabulary. The 11:40pm phone call in `HandToHeart` is
  deliberately kept as ordinary human worry, never danger.
- No fabricated scarcity. None of the nine use the pages' "Reserve" framing, and none repeats
  the rosary page's "Save up to 67%".
- No fabricated testimonials, names or ratings. First-person narration only. The necklace page
  itself states there are no reviews yet, so none were invented.
- No second-person assertions about the reader's faith or practice. Community framing or first
  person throughout. `NeverLearnedItProperly` keeps every admission of uncertainty inside the
  speaker's own experience, never the reader's.
- Written conservatively on ages and third-party devotion, because a previous ad in this
  account was rejected and the reason is unknown.
- **All prices verified against the live pages**, not from CLAUDE.md, which was stale.
- Blessing described only as "in the Benedictine tradition". No Rome, no named abbey, no
  chaplain.
