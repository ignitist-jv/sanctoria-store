## Compliance notes

- No em or en dashes anywhere.
- **Three of the six carry an explicit non-efficacy line**: "It doesn't do the praying",
  "It's a devotional piece, not a protective one", "A bracelet doesn't do anything on its
  own". On an account where efficacy is the main rejection risk, stating the limit outright
  is the strongest available position.
- No fear, curse or spiritual-warfare vocabulary. No fabricated scarcity or urgency.
- No fabricated testimonials, names or ratings.
- No second-person assertions about the reader's faith. Community framing throughout ("most
  Catholic men", "a lot of Catholics", "grandparents know this"). The one second-person
  construction, "if there's a Confirmation coming up in the family", is conditional and
  asserts nothing about the reader.
- **Dating in number 6 is accurate**: Benedict lived roughly 1500 years ago, the medal itself
  is much later, the familiar design was formalised in 1880, and the medal is described only
  as worn "for centuries".
- The primary texts deliberately do NOT repeat the spoken scripts. Most viewers scroll with
  sound off, so each one enters the angle from a different door and earns the click alone.
