## Keep (do not change what works)

- **Proven mechanism to carry:** a prayer that is sung keeps being prayed. A spoken prayer is said once and is gone; a song with her name in it gets played in the car, at the bedside, on the morning of the surgery, at the graveside, and every play is another prayer by name.
- **Proven emotion:** love of family and the wish for protection and peace. **Not** fear. The relaunch rule for this domain is absolute.
- **Proven hook structure:** an imperative naming the recipient in the first four words, then a product fact stated as a consequence. "Give a daughter a prayer song and every chorus sings her own name." No past-tense report of a customer, no reaction, no disbelief, no crying, no customer count.
- **Proven format:** PHOTO over UGC on this account. Narrow, calm, cream and bronze, Fraunces headline, gold CTA.
- **Customer phrases to carry:** by name. Prayed over. The empty chair. The kitchen table. Someone who has passed. Hand-blessed in the Benedictine tradition.
- **Banned on ads and page for this domain:** bad luck, curse, evil, hex, Jezebel, spiritual attack, spiritual warfare. Use peace, blessed, blessing, grace, protection, guard your family, faith, sacred, devotion.
