## YOUR DAILY BLESSING PRAYER

*Hold the St. Benedict Marian Bracelet in both hands, or rest your fingers on the beads at your wrist, and pray aloud:*

---

Heavenly Father, I come before You this morning
wearing the blessed bracelet of St. Benedict,
and I ask You to surround me and my household
with Your gentle and mighty protection.

Through the intercession of St. Benedict,
watch over my finances and keep my home in Your care.
Guard my body in health and my mind in peace.
Bless every relationship I hold dear
with patience, understanding, and love.

Pour Your peace into every room of my home,
into every hour of this day,
and into the quiet of tonight when I lay down to sleep.

You are my shield and my refuge.
Nothing that troubles the world can take root in a heart that is Yours.
I belong to Christ, and by His grace,
I am held, I am kept, I am at peace. Amen.

## VADE RETRO SATANA

*The Protection Prayer of St. Benedict*

**Latin:**
Vade retro Satana!
Nunquam suade mihi vana!
Sunt mala quae libas.
Ipse venena bibas!

**English:**
Step back, tempter.
Never lead me toward what is empty.
What you offer is worthless.
Keep it for yourself.

---

## CRUX SACRA SIT MIHI LUX

*The Cross Prayer of St. Benedict*

**Latin:**
Crux sacra sit mihi lux,
Non draco sit mihi dux.

**English:**
Let the Holy Cross be my light.
Let not the dragon be my guide.

## PRAYING WITH YOUR BLESSED BRACELET, A QUICK GUIDE

Follow these steps each morning with your St. Benedict Marian Bracelet. At each bead you will add a short St. Benedict intercession that most prayer guides leave out. This is what turns a daily prayer into a daily blessing.

---

**1. Hold the St. Benedict medal. Make the Sign of the Cross.**
Touch the medal to your forehead, chest, and shoulders. Say: "In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen." Pause. Breathe. You are entering sacred ground.

**2. Pray the Apostles' Creed.**
Still resting your fingers on the medal, profess your faith: "I believe in God, the Father Almighty, Creator of heaven and earth..."

**3. Pray the Our Father.**
Move to the first bead. Pray the Our Father slowly. Mean every word.

**4. Pray Three Hail Marys.**
Move through the next three beads. Each Hail Mary is for an increase of faith, hope, and charity in your life.

**5. Pray the Glory Be.**
"Glory be to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit. As it was in the beginning, is now, and ever shall be, world without end. Amen."

**6. Begin Each Set of Beads With a Blessing Intention.**
Before starting each set of beads, speak the intention aloud (listed below). Then pray one Our Father.

**7. At Each Our Father Bead, Add This St. Benedict Prayer:**
After each Our Father, before moving to the Hail Mary beads, say: *"St. Benedict, faithful servant of Christ, intercede for me now. Let every trouble be turned away. Let every snare be broken. Let the peace of God fill its place. Amen."*

**8. Pray the Hail Marys With Intention.**
Move through each bead slowly. Do not rush. Each bead is a stone laid in the wall of protection around your life.

**9. Close With the Hail Holy Queen.**
Pray: "Hail, Holy Queen, Mother of Mercy, our life, our sweetness, and our hope..."

**10. Final St. Benedict Blessing.**
Hold the medal on your bracelet and say: *"By the power of the Holy Cross, by the intercession of St. Benedict, and by the grace of Jesus Christ, I place my home, my family, my body, and my future in Your keeping. Your peace fills every corner. It is done. Amen."*

---

## DAILY BLESSING INTENTIONS

*Speak the intention aloud before beginning each set of beads.*

**One, Finances and Provision**
"Lord, I place my finances in Your care. Close every door of loss. Open every door of provision. Let Your goodness guard all that You have given."

**Two, Health and Body**
"Lord, I place my body in Your care. Restore my strength. Renew my rest. Keep me well for the work and the people You have entrusted to me."

**Three, Marriage and Relationships**
"Lord, I place my relationships in Your care. Remove every seed of division. Soften every hardened word. Let love and truth be the foundation of every bond I hold."

**Four, Children and Family**
"Lord, I place my children and my family in Your care. Cover them where I cannot reach. Guard them where I cannot see. Draw them closer to You with every passing day."

**Five, Peace, Sleep, and the Spirit of My Home**
"Lord, I place my peace in Your care. Fill every room of my home with Your presence. Guard my sleep tonight. Let Your light rest where I dwell."

---

*Pray daily. God is faithful. His peace remains.*
